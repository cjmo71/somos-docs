Android 0.9 is the same app idea as the iPhone app, built for Android. These are the places it differs.

## Different on Android

| On iPhone | On Android |
| --- | --- |
| TestFlight | Google Play closed test: [First Launch](/android/first-launch) |
| Local Network and Bluetooth permissions | Nearby devices (Android 17 or later) and notifications (Android 13 or later): [Permissions](/android/permissions) |
| Live Activity and Dynamic Island | A notification card on the Lock Screen and in the shade: [Lock Screen and Widgets](/android/lock-screen-and-widgets) |
| Home Screen widget | Three Android widgets: Presets, Mute, and Rooms & Volume |
| Sleep Timer and Alarms as sheets | A dialog for the Sleep Timer and Android's own time and day editor for alarms |
| Sliders in iOS style | Android's Material sliders. The controls and labels are the same |
| Whole Home purchase | No Whole Home purchase in the Android build. Every room has the full controls |

## Not on Android yet

- Apple Watch and watch faces (there is no Wear OS app)
- Siri and Shortcuts
- Syncing presets, room order and favorites between phones (iCloud sync on iPhone). On Android they stay on the phone you set them up on
- Demo Mode

## Good to know

- Switching to another playlist while the queue is still filling can leave one already-sent command behind. Wait for loading to finish first.
- Under heavy load, a volume change or acknowledgement from the speaker can arrive late.
