Somos InControl for Android gives you the same single, focused page for each of your Sonos® rooms that the iPhone app has: volume, EQ, sound presets, home theater controls and your music, over your local Wi-Fi, with no Somos account and no cloud round-trip. Android 0.11 is in a closed test on Google Play.

<Note>
  This tab follows the iPhone manual page for page and only says what is different on Android. Everything it doesn't mention works as the iPhone page describes, in Android's own look. The pictures on the iPhone pages are iPhone pictures.
</Note>

## What works the same

  <Card title="Volume" icon="volume-high" href="/features/volume">

  <Card title="Sound Presets" icon="star" href="/features/sound-presets">

  <Card title="EQ and Trueplay" icon="sliders" href="/features/eq-and-trueplay">
    Bass, treble, loudness, Trueplay, balance, subwoofer, surround and height, wherever the speaker has them.

  <Card title="Home Theater" icon="tv" href="/features/home-theater">
    TV / Music source, audio-format badge, Night Sound, Speech Boost, the room diagram and assigning surrounds, fronts and the Sub.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Music" icon="music" href="/features/music">
    Favorites, Playlists, Queue and your music services, played straight to the room.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rooms and groups" icon="house" href="/rooms/overview">

## What is different

- **Install** is through Google Play's closed test, not TestFlight: [First Launch on Android](/android/first-launch).
- **Permissions** are Nearby devices and notifications, not Local Network and Bluetooth: [Android Permissions](/android/permissions).
- **The Lock Screen card is a notification, and the widgets are Android widgets**: [Lock Screen and Widgets](/android/lock-screen-and-widgets).
- **Some iPhone features have no Android version yet**: [What is different on Android](/android/differences).
