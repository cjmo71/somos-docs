Somos InControl plays your music services straight to a room: **Apple Music, Amazon Music, Pandora, Spotify, iHeartRadio, TuneIn and Bandcamp**. Each one you have set up shows as a button at the top of the [music page](/features/music); tap it, browse, and play. The speaker streams from the service itself — your phone only tells it what to play.

<Note>
  Playing needs the service on your Sonos® system too. If you tap something and the music page says the service **isn't set up on your Sonos system yet**, add it in the Sonos® app once, then try again.
</Note>

## Setting up a service

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Music Services page">
    On the music page, tap **Add** at the end of the service row (or **Add a music service** when nothing is set up yet). The page lists the services with a button beside each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Connect">
    The service's own sign-in page opens inside Somos. Sign in as you normally would — Somos never sees your password. Some services show a short code to enter on their page; the row tells you when. Come back to Somos; once the service confirms, the row's button reads **Disconnect** and the service appears in the row on the music page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apple Music needs no Connect">
    Apple only lets its own Sonos® app sign in to Apple Music, so Somos does not offer a Connect step — the row simply reads **Plays from your Sonos favourites**. Link Apple Music to your household in the Sonos® app itself, and Somos plays what is already there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="TuneIn needs nothing">
    Services that need no account, such as TuneIn, read **No sign-in needed** and are ready as soon as they are on your Sonos® system.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Signing in here links the service to your Sonos® system the same way the Sonos® app does. Somos keeps the sign-in on your iPhone, in the device's own secure storage, and only for that Sonos® system.

## Browsing, searching and playing

- **Tap a service button** and the music page slides down to a handle; the service's own page opens above it — shelves, rows and grids drawn the way the service asks, with album art and station logos.
- **Open albums, playlists and folders in place.** A single back row at the top takes you up one level.
- **Search** sits at the top of every service page. Type, and the service's own categories appear — pick songs, albums, artists, playlists or stations. Results replace the page until you clear the field.
- **Tap to play.** A song, album, playlist or station starts on the room and the player comes back up. The **plus** on a song adds it to the queue instead; the **heart** keeps it in [Favorites](/features/favorites-and-queue).
- **The lit button is the service that is sounding** — it lights when you play from it, when you play a favorite from it, and stays lit across rooms and after you close and reopen the page.

## Apple Music

Somos cannot sign in to Apple Music or browse its catalogue — Apple allows only its own Sonos® app to do that. Set Apple Music up in the Sonos® app and Somos plays what is already linked there. Songs playing from Apple Music also show quality badges on the player (**Lossless**, **Hi-Res Lossless**, **Dolby Atmos**) — what Apple's catalogue offers for that track, not what the speaker is decoding.

## Disconnecting a service

Press and hold the service's button on the music page and choose **Disconnect**, or tap **Disconnect** on the Music Services page. This forgets the sign-in in Somos only — your Sonos® system keeps its own account for the service, and the Sonos® app is unaffected. Apple Music has no Connect or Disconnect — there is nothing here for Somos to link. The one related iPhone permission is for the quality badges, which you manage in **Settings → Somos InControl**.

## If something does not play

- **"… isn't set up on your Sonos system yet"** — the Sonos® system has no account for that service. Add it in the Sonos® app, then try again.
- **The wrong room plays** — Somos plays to the room whose music page you have open; check the room name under **Music** at the top of the page.
- **The service list will not load** — Somos asks the room that leads the group. Try again from a room that is not part of a home theater, or use **Add** again; the page re-reads on every open.
- **A song is missing from Apple Music's catalogue** — a library-only song cannot be sent to the speaker, and the page says so.

<Tip>
  Which services should come next? Tap **Poll: your favorite music services** in the menu, just under Rooms, and tell the developer.
</Tip>
