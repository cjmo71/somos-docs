Somos InControl ships with a Home Screen widget and a set of Siri shortcuts, so the controls you reach for most don't need the app open at all. Both talk to your speakers over the same local Wi-Fi connection as the app.

## Home Screen widget

The **Somos InControl** widget comes in **small**, **medium** and **large** sizes. It's built from rows, and each row is one of five types:

- **TV presets** — a room's three TV preset buttons, which apply the saved sound directly
- **Music presets** — the same for the room's three music presets
- **Volume + mute** — the room name with **−**, **Mute** and **+** buttons
- **Set volume** — one button, **Set to** and a number, that sets the room to the volume you chose in the widget's editor
- **Favourite groups** — your saved groups, one tap to rebuild each

The Home Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts are free; they are not part of [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home).

**Small** holds one or two buttons, **medium** holds one or two rows, and **large** holds three rows in any combination — so a large widget can show TV presets for the living room, music presets for the kitchen, and volume for the bedroom.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the widget">
    Long-press the Home Screen, tap **+**, search for **Somos InControl**, choose a size and tap **Add Widget**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what each row shows">
    Long-press the widget and tap **Edit Widget**. For each row pick a **Type** and, for anything but favourite groups, a **Room**. For a **Set volume** row, also choose the **Volume** number its button sets. The editor only asks about the rows the chosen size can hold.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the app once">
    Open Somos InControl at least once after installing so the widget has your rooms and presets — it refreshes every time you open the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Preset buttons apply exactly what the preset captured, including volume if you ticked it. The active preset lights up so you can see which one is loaded.
</Note>

## Siri and Shortcuts

The phrases below work as soon as the app is installed — nothing to set up in the Shortcuts app.

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Apply **Movies** in Somos InControl" · "Switch to **Movies** in Somos InControl" | Applies the preset with that name to its room. Siri knows every preset by the name you gave it. |
| "Turn up the volume on Somos InControl" · "Volume up on Somos InControl" | Raises the current room's volume by 2 |
| "Turn down the volume on Somos InControl" · "Lower the volume on Somos InControl" | Lowers the current room's volume by 2 |
| "Switch Somos InControl room" · "Next room on Somos InControl" | Moves to the next room |

The same actions — **Apply Preset**, **Volume Up**, **Volume Down**, **Switch Room** — and a new one, **Set Volume**, are available in the Shortcuts app for automations and Home Screen shortcuts. Search for Somos InControl when adding an action. **Set Volume** sets a room to an exact volume number: pick the room and the number, and use it in an automation or on the Home Screen.

<Tip>
  Because presets are matched by name, give them distinct names. Two rooms each with a preset called "Movies" will make Siri ask which one you mean.
</Tip>
