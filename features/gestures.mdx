Somos InControl has two gestures you can switch on. Both are **off by default**, so nothing changes until you choose it, and with them off the pages scroll, swipe between rooms and tap exactly as before.

## Turning them on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Tap the menu button in the top-left corner of any room page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open System settings">
    Under **Rooms**, tap **System settings** to unfold it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on what you want">
    **Pinch for volume** and **Swipe art to change songs** are the last two switches before **Open iOS Settings for Somos**. Each works on its own; turn on one or both.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pinch for volume

With it on, put **two fingers anywhere on a room page or the music page**: **spread them apart to turn the volume up, pinch them together to turn it down.** The change is relative to where the volume was when your fingers landed, a **Volume N%** pill floats at the top with the live number while you pinch and fades after you let go, and you feel a light tap at each step. The slider still works exactly as before — this is an extra way in, not a replacement.

## Swipe art to change songs

With it on, **swipe the album art on the player right for the next song, left to go back to the previous one** — one song per swipe, with a tap of haptic and a small nudge of the art (no nudge when Reduce Motion is on). The swipe has to be a clear sideways one: a short drag does nothing, a vertical drag still scrolls the list under the art, and a tap does nothing new.

<Note>
  Earlier 36 test builds had a two-finger swipe for tracks. It is gone; the art swipe and this switch replace it. Pinch for volume is unchanged.
</Note>
