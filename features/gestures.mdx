Somos InControl has one gesture, and it works everywhere automatically — there is nothing to turn on. With it, the pages still scroll, swipe between rooms and tap exactly as before.

## Pinch for volume

Put **two fingers anywhere on a room page or the music page**: **spread them apart to turn the volume up, pinch them together to turn it down.** The change is relative to where the volume was when your fingers landed, a **Volume N%** pill floats at the top with the live number while you pinch and fades after you let go, and you feel a light tap at each step. The slider still works exactly as before — this is an extra way in, not a replacement.
