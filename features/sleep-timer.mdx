Fall asleep to music, or let the TV go quiet on its own. The sleep timer stops the room playing after the time you pick. It is your Sonos® speaker's own timer, so it keeps running when Somos is closed or your iPhone is locked.

## Set a sleep timer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the room">
    The timer is for the room you have open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Tap the menu button in the top-left corner. **Sleep timer** has its own row, just under **Rooms**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a length">
    Tap **Sleep timer**. The pop-up has **Off**, **15 min**, **30 min**, **45 min**, **60 min** and **90 min**. Tap one and the timer starts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To stop it early, open the pop-up again and tap **Off**. To change it, tap another length.

When the timer ends, the room stops playing. The sound stops; it does not fade out.

## See the time left

While a timer runs, you can see how long is left in three places:

- **On the menu row** — **Sleep timer** shows the minutes left.
- **In the pop-up** — the time left sits at the top, above the choices.
- **On the room page** — a small moon and the minutes, for example **☾ 23m**. On a soundbar it sits on the source row, right after the connection (**TV · eARC ☾ 23m**, for example). A room without a soundbar shows it on the row with the **Music** button. The music page shows it too. The moon is yellow and the minutes are grey. At 5 minutes or less, the minutes turn red.

## Grouped rooms

Sonos® keeps one sleep timer for a whole group. Set it on any room in the group and the whole group stops together.

## On your Apple Watch

The **Sleep Timer** complication shows the time left on your watch face, or **Off**. Tap it and the Watch app opens on the timer choices: **Off** and the same five lengths. The time left shows at the top, with the same yellow moon, and turns red at 5 minutes or less. See [Watch Face](/features/watch-face).

<Note>
  The timer lives on the speaker, so a timer set in the Sonos® app shows here too, and one set here shows in the Sonos® app.
</Note>
