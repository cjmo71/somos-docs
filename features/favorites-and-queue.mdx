Two small buttons do most of the work on the [music page](/features/music): the **heart** keeps something for later, and the **plus** adds a song to what is coming up next. Both sit on every song row and tile while you browse a music service, and both sit beside the title on the player while a song from a service is playing — a station gets the heart only.

## Favorites

Tap the **heart** on any song, album, playlist or station from a music service and it is saved to your Favorites. Tap the heart again to let it go. The heart is not offered on things that cannot be played on their own, such as a category or an artist.

### The Favorites page

The **Favorites** chip sits with **Playlists** and **Queue** in a fixed row above the music services on the music page — the services scroll in their own row underneath, these three stay put. Tap it and the player slides down to its handle, and the Favorites page opens where a service's page would. Tap the lit **Favorites** chip again to scroll back to the top of the page.

- **What is playing, at the top.** The first row shows the art, the song, the artist and the room — **Now playing · Bedroom**, **Paused · Bedroom**, or **Last played** for what the room had on before. A long title scrolls so you can read all of it. When nothing is on, the row says **Nothing playing in** that room. Tap it to bring the player back up.
- **Import from Sonos Favorites** — the first row of the list. It brings over what you saved in the Sonos® app (see below).
- **Favorites from our services** — everything you hearted, newest first, with its art, where it came from and what kind of thing it is. Long titles wrap onto two lines. **Every row has a lit heart at the end** — tap it to let that favorite go, the same as the heart everywhere else.
- **Tap one to play it.** It plays on the room, the service it came from lights up in the row, and the player comes back up. A radio station shows the station's name and what is on right now, the way the station reports it.
- **Play all** — the button beside the **Favorites from our services** heading. It empties the room's queue, adds every hearted **song** in the order you see on the page, and starts the first one. Stations, albums and playlists cannot go in a queue, so they are skipped, and the message says how many: **Playing 4 of 6 favorites in Bedroom · 2 skipped**. Shuffle is turned off so the order is the page's order.
- **Swipe left to remove it** — a shortcut for the heart.
- Song, album and playlist rows have their own **plus**, so you can queue a favorite or add it to a playlist instead of playing it.

With the player up, its handle reads **Hide player** — tap it to lower the player onto the Favorites page. These are the favorites you saved in Somos; nothing on this page is stored in your Sonos® system. Signed in to iCloud, they follow your Apple ID to your other devices, along with your playlists, and they are shared with no one else. See [iCloud Sync](/features/icloud-sync).

### Bring your Sonos® favorites and playlists into Somos

Favorites you saved in the Sonos® app are a separate list, and they do not appear on their own. To copy them over, tap **Import from Sonos Favorites** at the top of the Favorites page. Somos reads your Sonos® favorites and adds each one it doesn't already have; a message says how many came over and how many couldn't (**Imported 5 Sonos favorites · 0 failed.**). An imported favorite plays exactly as it was saved in Sonos®. The **Playlists** page has a matching **Import from Sonos Playlists** row, which copies your Sonos® playlists in as Somos playlists.

The import only copies. It never changes your Sonos® system, and there is no option to send a Somos favorite or playlist back to it. The [Apple Watch app](/features/apple-watch) still plays your Sonos® favorites directly.

## Add to queue

The **plus** adds a song to the end of the queue without changing what is playing — a short **Added to the queue** toast, a tap of haptic, and the queue grows. You will find it:

- on every **song** row or tile while browsing or searching a service (albums, playlists and stations open or play instead — they are not queued from here),
- on a **song** on the Favorites page,
- on the **player**, right after the heart — when what is playing is a song. Here the plus opens a short menu (see below) with **Add to Queue** as its first choice. While a radio station or a stream is playing, the player shows the heart alone: a station has no end, so it cannot be queued.

If the speaker refuses — the service is not set up on your Sonos® system, or the song is not in Apple Music's catalogue — the toast says so.

## Add the playing song to a playlist

Tap the **plus** beside the heart on the player while a song is playing. A short menu opens:

- **Add to Queue** — adds the song to the end of the queue, as the plus always did.
- **Add to Playlist** — opens your **Playlists** page with the heading **Add "the song" to…**. Tap a playlist to add the song to it; a song that is already in it says so instead of going in twice.
- **Create Playlist** — name a new Somos playlist, and the song goes straight into it.

A short message confirms where it went (**Added to** and the playlist's name). You can also press and hold the heart or the plus for the same menu. The menu has no *Add to Sonos…* choices — use the import rows above to bring Sonos® favorites and playlists into Somos.

## The queue

The **Queue** chip, next to **Favorites** and **Playlists**, opens the queue, and it holds only the queue. The current track is highlighted; tap a track to jump to it, swipe left to remove it, tap **Queue** in the list's heading to fold the list away, tap **Save** to save it as a Somos playlist (the app asks for a name), and tap **Clear** to empty it — the app asks before it does. With nothing queued, the page says **The queue is empty. Play music or add a song to put it here.** Tap the lit **Queue** chip again to scroll back to the top.

Every song in the queue that came from one of your music services has a **heart** at the end of its row. Tap it to keep the song in your Favorites, and again to let it go; it is filled while the song is kept. Press and hold the row and tap **Add to playlist** to put that song in one of your playlists.

<Tip>
  If you started something and the queue is not what you expected, the player's **Play** resumes the queue where you left it; when the queue changed while the TV was on, the page tells you it is starting from the top.
</Tip>
