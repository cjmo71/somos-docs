Sound presets let you lock in exactly how a room should sound for a given situation — movie night, background music, late-night listening — and bring it all back instantly with a single tap. Every room has its own presets: three on the room page (TV presets) and three more on the music page (music presets).

## Where the presets live

- **Room page** — the **Sound Presets** row sits between the volume card and the EQ card. These three slots are the room's _TV_ presets and carry a small TV badge once saved. On a soundbar, while **Music** is lit, the room page keeps only its header and volume — the presets you see then are the music ones, on the music page. Tap **TV** and the TV presets are back.
- **Music page** — tap **Music** in the source row at the top of a room to open it. It has its own **Sound Presets** row with three _music_ slots (music-note badge), which can also capture the Music Level and Ambient/Full setting that only exist on that page.
- **Lock Screen** — a TV room's three presets are also chips on the [Live Activity](/features/live-activity) card, by name, with the loaded one lit. Tap one there and the room page follows.

All six start out named **Preset 1**, **Preset 2** and **Preset 3**. A preset is stored per room — saving "Movies" in the Living Room does not change the Bedroom's presets.

## What a preset captures

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Always saved">
    The sound itself is always captured — you cannot leave these out:

    - **Bass** and **Treble**
    - **Surround** level (rooms with surrounds)
    - **Height** level (height-capable soundbars and Era 300 satellites)
    - **Subwoofer** level (rooms with a Sub)
    - **Crossover** (rooms with a Sub whose speaker reports one)
    - On the Music sheet only: **Music Level** and **Ambient / Full** playback mode
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Only if you tick them">
    The save sheet has an **Also save** list. Anything left unticked is simply not part of the preset, and loading it leaves that setting exactly as the room has it now:

    - **Volume**
    - **Night Sound** (soundbar rooms, room page only)
    - **Speech Boost** (soundbar rooms, room page only)
    - **Loudness**

    An **All** row at the bottom ticks or clears every option offered for that room. The list only shows what the room can actually use — a music preset never offers Night Sound or Speech Boost, and a room without Loudness does not offer Loudness.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Saving, loading, and renaming presets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dial in your sound">
    Adjust the EQ sliders and toggles until the room sounds exactly the way you want. If you want the preset to set the volume too, set that as well.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold a preset to save">
    Press and hold any preset card for about half a second. The **Save Preset** sheet opens with the name field (up to 14 characters) and the **Also save** tick list. Tick what you want the preset to carry, then tap **Save**. The card highlights as the active preset and a toast confirms "*name* saved".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a preset to load it">
    Tap any card to apply everything it captured at once. The sliders move to the saved values and the speaker updates immediately. If the preset captured a volume, the room's volume changes too — so keep that in mind before ticking Volume on a preset you use late at night.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename without re-saving">
    Hold the card again, change only the name and leave the ticks alone — the button reads **Rename** instead of **Save**, and every stored value is kept exactly as it was. Change any tick and it becomes a full **Save** again, re-capturing the room's current settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The tick list opens pre-filled with what the preset already holds, so a rename never silently drops Volume, Night Sound, Speech Boost or Loudness. Unticking something is a deliberate choice and clears it on Save.
</Note>

## The highlight stays until you change the sound

The preset you loaded stays highlighted when you leave the room and come back, switch rooms, or close and reopen the app — the room remembers which one it is on, whether you loaded it on the page, from the widget or from the Lock Screen. It clears in three cases:

- you move a slider the preset captured, or flip Night Sound, Speech Boost or Loudness on a preset that captured them — a toast says "_name_ preset was disabled", and your change stays in effect;
- a fresh read from the speaker no longer matches the preset's sound values — for example after a change in the official Sonos® app;
- you load another preset, which takes the highlight with it.

To bring a preset back, tap it again.

## Presets in music-only rooms

Presets work in every room, not just soundbar rooms. In a room without a soundbar, Night Sound and Speech Boost are never offered, so the presets there capture the EQ levels plus, optionally, Volume and Loudness.

## Presets from Siri and the widget

Once you name a preset, Siri knows it by that name — say "**Apply Movies in Somos InControl**" and it loads without any Shortcut setup. The Home Screen widget can also apply presets with one tap. See [Widget & Siri](/features/widget-and-siri).

<Tip>
  A good starting set for a soundbar room: **Movies** (Speech Boost on, a little extra bass), **Late Night** (Night Sound on, Volume ticked at a low level), and **Music** saved from the music page with Loudness on. Name them — the names are what you see on the Lock Screen card.
</Tip>
