Sound presets let you lock in exactly how a room should sound for a given situation — movie night, background music, late-night listening — and bring it all back instantly with a single tap. Each preset is a complete snapshot of the room's sound at the moment you save it, so you never have to re-dial the same settings twice.

## What a preset captures

When you save a preset, it stores the current values of:

- **Bass** level
- **Treble** level
- **Night Sound** toggle state (on or off)
- **Speech Boost** toggle state (on or off)

## Saving, loading, and renaming presets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dial in your sound">
    Adjust the EQ sliders, Night Sound, and Speech Boost until the room sounds exactly the way you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold a preset slot to save">
    Press and hold any preset to write your current sound settings into it. The preset slot updates immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a preset to load it">
    Tap any saved preset to apply everything it captured at once — EQ levels and toggle states all change together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold again to rename">
    Press and hold a saved preset a second time to rename it. Give it a name that makes its purpose obvious, like "Movies" or "Late Night".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When a preset deactivates

A preset stays active as long as none of its captured controls have changed. The moment you manually adjust any setting the preset owns — a slider, Night Sound, or Speech Boost — the preset deactivates automatically. A brief notification explains what happened, and your manual change stays in effect. To re-activate it, tap the preset again.

## Presets work in every room

Presets are available in both soundbar (home theater) rooms and music-only rooms. In rooms without a soundbar, Night Sound and Speech Boost are not present, so saved presets for those rooms capture EQ levels only.

<Tip>
  Consider saving four distinct presets per room: one tuned for **movies** (boosted dialogue), one for **music** (balanced EQ), one for **late-night** (Night Sound on, lower treble), and one for **kids' TV** (Speech Boost on, conservative bass). Switching between them takes one tap.
</Tip>
