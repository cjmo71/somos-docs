Live Activity brings Somos InControl's volume controls out of the app and onto your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island — so you can raise or lower the volume in your current room without picking up your phone, unlocking it, or switching apps. It shows the current volume percentage and two buttons: **+** to increase and **−** to decrease.

## What Live Activity shows

- The **current volume percentage** for the selected room, updated as it changes
- A **+** button to increase volume
- A **−** button to decrease volume
- Displayed on the **Lock Screen** and in the **Dynamic Island** (on supported iPhone models)

## Enabling Live Activity

Live Activity is off by default to respect iOS battery and notification preferences. Turn it on whenever you want it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Tap the **menu button** in the top-left corner of the app to open the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Live Activity">
    Find the **Live Activity** toggle and switch it on. The Lock Screen and Dynamic Island widget appear immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust volume from anywhere">
    Lock your phone. The volume control is now visible on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island — tap **+** or **−** to change the room's volume without unlocking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Live Activity is the fastest way to adjust volume during a movie or music session — glance at your phone, tap once, done. No Face ID, no app switching, no waking the screen fully.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Live Activity always reflects the **currently selected room** in the app. If you switch rooms inside Somos InControl, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island controls update to match the new room.
</Note>
