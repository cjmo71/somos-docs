Live Activity brings Somos InControl's most-used controls out of the app and onto your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island — so you can adjust the current room without unlocking your phone or switching apps.

{/* screenshot needed: the Lock Screen card and the Dynamic Island in the 40.0.5 dark navy (the pictures on this page are the 40.0.2 navy) */}

## What Live Activity shows

The card follows what the speaker is really doing — TV on the wire means the TV card, even if the music page is open in the app. Since build 40.0.2 it is drawn in the same navy as the Home Screen widget; from build 40.0.5 that is a dark navy on the Lock Screen card, the widgets and the colored parts of the Dynamic Island, with bigger symbols and labels that wrap:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="TV (soundbar) mode">
    - The **current volume percentage**, large, beside the TV symbol and the room name with **· TV**
    - The audio format as badges under the room name — **DOLBY ATMOS** and **EARC**, for example — when the soundbar reports them
    - A volume slider with **−** and **\+** buttons
    - The room's **three sound presets by name**, then **Mute**. The loaded preset is lit like the app's picked buttons. Tap one to apply it; the room page follows at once if it is open. A room with no saved presets shows no preset chips.
    - **Mute** is a grey button with a slashed speaker, reading **Muted**, while the room is muted
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Music mode">
    - The room's **album art** beside the track title, then the **artist** and, when the service reports one, the format as a badge — **LOSSLESS**, for example. The album is left off so the format always fits on the line.
    - While the music is paused, the card's header reads **Standby**
    - Elapsed and remaining time
    - **Previous**, **Play/Pause** and **Next** buttons
    - A volume slider with **−** and **+** buttons, and the room's music presets underneath
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  The Night Sound and Speech Boost chips from earlier builds are gone. Save them into a preset instead — a preset can carry Night Sound and Speech Boost — and switch between presets from the card. See [Sound Presets](/features/sound-presets).
</Note>

In the **Dynamic Island** (iPhone 14 Pro and later), the compact view shows the TV symbol and the audio format for a TV room, or the room's album art for music, on one side, and **Vol** with the volume percentage on the other. Press and hold it to expand it: the room (with the album art first on music), what is playing, and the same buttons as the card. On iOS 18 and later, a paired Apple Watch's Smart Stack shows the card as a read-only glance on the same navy: three lines — the room, the song and artist (or **TV**), and the audio format — with a volume ring and, on music, the album art. Tap it and the [Watch app](/features/apple-watch) opens on that room.

## Enabling Live Activity

Live Activity is **off by default**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Tap the **menu button** in the top-left corner of the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the Live Activity switch">
    Tap **App**, then **Somos settings**, and switch **Live Activity** on. The card appears for the room you currently have open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow Live Activities in iOS Settings if prompted">
    If iOS has Live Activities disabled for the app, an orange warning appears under the switch — tap it to jump to **Settings → Apps → Somos InControl → Live Activities** and turn them on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust from anywhere">
    Lock your phone. The card is on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island — tap **−** or **+**, mute, switch presets, or skip tracks without unlocking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Live Activity always reflects the **currently selected room** in the app. Switch rooms inside Somos InControl and the card switches with you. It ends when you quit the app, or about 30 minutes after you send the app to the background, so a stale card never sits on your Lock Screen. Open the app again to bring it back.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Prefer something permanent on the Home Screen? The [widget](/features/widget-and-siri) gives you presets, volume and mute for up to two rooms without opening the app at all.
</Tip>
