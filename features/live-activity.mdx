Live Activity brings Somos InControl's most-used controls out of the app and onto your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island — so you can adjust the current room without unlocking your phone or switching apps. It requires iOS 16.2 or later; the buttons on the card need iOS 17.

## What Live Activity shows

The card follows what the speaker is really doing — TV on the wire means the TV card, even if the music page is open in the app:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="TV (soundbar) mode">
    - The room name, with the audio format it is receiving underneath (**Dolby Atmos · eARC** and so on — just **TV** until the format arrives)
    - A **Mute** chip, then the room's **three sound presets by name** — the loaded one lit. Tap one to apply it; the room page follows at once if it is open. A room with no saved presets shows no preset chips.
    - The **current volume percentage** with **−** and **\+** buttons
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Music mode">
    - The track title, then **artist — album** and, when the service reports one, the format (**Lossless**, **Hi-Res Lossless · Dolby Atmos**)
    - Elapsed and remaining time
    - **Previous**, **Play/Pause** and **Next** buttons
    - The **current volume percentage** with **−** and **+** buttons
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  The Night Sound and Speech Boost chips from earlier builds are gone. Save them into a preset instead — a preset can carry Night Sound and Speech Boost — and switch between presets from the card. See [Sound Presets](/features/sound-presets).
</Note>

In the **Dynamic Island** (iPhone 14 Pro and later), the compact view shows the Somos mark on one side and the volume percentage on the other; press and hold it to expand to the full card with the same buttons. On iOS 18, a paired Apple Watch's Smart Stack shows a read-only glance card with the room and volume.

## Enabling Live Activity

Live Activity is **off by default**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Tap the **menu button** in the top-left corner of the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the Live Activity switch">
    Under **Rooms**, open **System settings** and switch **Live Activity** on. The card appears for the room you currently have open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow Live Activities in iOS Settings if prompted">
    If iOS has Live Activities disabled for the app, an orange warning appears under the switch — tap it to jump to **Settings → Apps → Somos InControl → Live Activities** and turn them on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust from anywhere">
    Lock your phone. The card is on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island — tap **−** or **+**, mute, switch presets, or skip tracks without unlocking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Live Activity always reflects the **currently selected room** in the app. Switch rooms inside Somos InControl and the card switches with you. It ends when you quit the app, or about 30 minutes after you send the app to the background, so a stale card never sits on your Lock Screen. Open the app again to bring it back.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Prefer something permanent on the Home Screen? The [widget](/features/widget-and-siri) gives you presets, volume and mute for up to two rooms without opening the app at all.
</Tip>
