Your Sonos alarms, in one list. **Menu › Alarms** shows every alarm on your system: the time, the room and the days. Add one, change one, or switch one on or off without opening the Sonos® app.

Alarms live on your Sonos® speakers, not in Somos. They ring with Somos closed, and they show in the Sonos® app too.

## See your alarms

Open the menu and tap **Alarms**. Each row shows the time, the room and the days it repeats. Pull down to read the list again. With no alarms, the page says **No alarms yet** and offers **Add an alarm**.

<Note>
  The **Alarms** row is not shown in Demo Mode, where no speaker can keep an alarm.
</Note>

## Add or edit an alarm

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the plus, or tap an alarm">
    The **+** at the top adds a new alarm. Tapping a row edits it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the room, time and days">
    **Room** is the room the alarm plays in. It plays in that room only, never in grouped rooms. Set the **Time** on the wheel and tick the **Days** it repeats.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what plays">
    **Plays** is the chime, or one of your Sonos® Favorites. An alarm plays for 1 hour, or until you stop it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the volume and save">
    Set the **Volume**, keep **Alarm on** switched on, and tap **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove an alarm, swipe it left and tap **Delete**.

<Note>
  An alarm marked **Made in the Sonos app · on / off only** can be switched on or off here, but not edited or deleted. Change it in the Sonos® app.
</Note>

## Switch an alarm on or off

Use the alarm's own switch in the list, or **Alarm on** when you edit it. An alarm that is off stays in the list.

## On your Apple Watch

The Watch app has an **Alarms** row with an on / off switch for each alarm. The watch does not add or edit alarms; do that on the iPhone. See [Apple Watch](/features/apple-watch).
