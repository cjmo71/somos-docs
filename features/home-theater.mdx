When Somos InControl detects a room built around a soundbar, it unlocks a dedicated set of home theater controls that only make sense in that context. These controls appear automatically in soundbar rooms and are hidden everywhere else, so your music-only rooms stay uncluttered.

## What every room gets

All rooms — single speakers, stereo pairs, and soundbar setups — share the same foundation:

- Volume slider and mute
- Bass and treble EQ
- Subwoofer slider (if a sub is bonded)
- Loudness toggle
- Sound presets
- Trueplay status and toggle
- Music playback controls

## Home-theater-only controls

Soundbar rooms add a second layer of controls on top of the above:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Height channel level" icon="layers">
    Sets the volume of the height (Atmos) channel relative to the main channels. Raise it to make overhead audio more prominent, lower it to blend it back in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Speech Boost" icon="message-circle">
    Enhances dialogue clarity so voices cut through effects and music. Particularly useful for TV programmes with dense soundtracks or quiet speech.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Night Sound" icon="moon">
    Compresses the loudest moments — explosions, music peaks — so you can follow the action without waking anyone. Designed for late-night viewing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="TV source" icon="tv">
    Switches the soundbar to its TV input, the same as pressing the input button on a remote. Useful when the soundbar has drifted to another source.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audio-format badge" icon="badge-info">
    Displays the audio format the soundbar is currently receiving — for example, Dolby Atmos, Dolby Digital, or PCM. Lets you confirm your TV or source device is sending the signal you expect.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Subwoofer in non-soundbar rooms

<Note>
  The subwoofer slider is not exclusive to home theater rooms. It appears in **any** room that has a subwoofer bonded — including stereo pairs and single-speaker rooms. If you have a sub paired with a music speaker, the slider shows up there too.
</Note>
