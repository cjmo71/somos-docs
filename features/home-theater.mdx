When Somos InControl detects a room built around a soundbar (Arc, Arc Ultra, Beam, Ray, Playbar, Playbase), it unlocks a set of home theater controls that only make sense in that context. They appear automatically in soundbar rooms and are hidden everywhere else, so your music-only rooms stay uncluttered.

## What every room gets

All rooms — single speakers, stereo pairs, portables and soundbar setups — share the same foundation:

- Volume slider with − / + and mute
- Bass and treble EQ, Loudness
- Subwoofer level, on/off, and crossover (when a Sub is bonded and the speaker reports one)
- Sound presets (three TV, three music)
- Trueplay, Status Light and Touch Controls tiles
- The music page with your music services, favorites and the queue
- A room diagram, room grouping, and room rename

## Home-theater-only controls

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="TV / Music source pills" icon="tv">
    At the top of a soundbar's page, a source row shows **TV** and **Music** pills. Tap **TV** to send the soundbar back to its TV input — the same as pressing the input button on a remote. Tap **Music** to open the music page and switch the bar to music; the TV audio is parked until you tap TV again. The pills follow what the speaker is really doing, so music started elsewhere lights **Music** too.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audio-format badge" icon="badge-check">
    Next to the source you'll see the connection (HDMI ARC, eARC, Optical) and the format the soundbar is decoding right now — Dolby Atmos, Dolby Digital 5.1, DTS, PCM Stereo and so on. It updates in real time, so you can confirm your TV is actually sending what you expect. While **Music** is lit, the same spot scrolls the format the music service reports for the track (**Lossless**, **Hi-Res Lossless**, **Dolby Atmos**) when there is one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Night Sound" icon="moon">
    Compresses the loudest moments — explosions, music peaks — so you can follow the action without waking anyone. Save it into a preset and it is one tap away on the [Live Activity](/features/live-activity) too.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Speech Boost" icon="comment">
    Lifts dialogue above effects and music. On most soundbars it's an on/off tile; on the **Arc Ultra** it's a five-step slider (Off, Low, Medium, High, Max — Medium is roughly the old "on"). If the speaker refuses the change, the switch goes back and the app tells you instead of pretending it worked.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Surround level and on/off" icon="speaker">
    When surrounds are bonded: a **Surround** slider (−15 to +15) sets how loud the rears play for TV, and a **Surrounds** switch mutes them without unbonding. Music has its own **Music Level** and **Ambient / Full** controls on the music page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Height channel" icon="arrow-up">
    Sets the level of the overhead (Atmos) channels (−10 to +10). Shown only where there is real height hardware — the Arc family's up-firing drivers, the Beam (Gen 2), or Era 300 satellites — never on a Ray or Playbar.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subwoofer" icon="volume-high">
    Level (−15 to +15), an on/off switch, and — where the speaker reports one — the crossover frequency (50–110 Hz). A second Sub, including two older-generation Subs, is supported.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Group Audio Delay" icon="clock">
    Appears only while the soundbar is grouped with other rooms. Steps the delay applied to the other rooms so they don't echo against the bar — the same setting as the Sonos® app's Group Audio Delay.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The room page while music plays

With **Music** lit, a soundbar's room page keeps only its header and the volume card — the TV presets, the EQ card, the toggles and the room diagram step aside, because the sound you are hearing is music, and music's presets and EQ live on the [music page](/features/music). Tap **TV**, or wait for the music to stop, and the page is exactly as it was. Rooms without a soundbar are unchanged.

## Building the home theater

Press and hold the room diagram on a soundbar's page to open **Home Theater Setup**, where you assign speakers to Front Left, Front Right, Surround Left, Surround Right, Subwoofer and Subwoofer 2 — including separate front speakers, which the official app doesn't offer. See [Speaker Setup](/rooms/speaker-setup) for the full walkthrough.

## Subwoofer in non-soundbar rooms

<Note>
  The subwoofer controls are not exclusive to home theater rooms. They appear in **any** room that has a Sub bonded — including stereo pairs and single-speaker rooms. A Sub that isn't bonded to anything gets its own page explaining that there's nothing to control until you bond it to a soundbar or stereo pair.
</Note>
