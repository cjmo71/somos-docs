The Volume card sits front and centre in Somos InControl — it's the first thing you see when you open a room, and every change you make applies to your Sonos® speaker the moment you make it. No confirmation step, no delay.

## Adjusting the volume

Drag the slider left to lower the volume or right to raise it. The change goes straight to the speaker over your local Wi-Fi as you drag — you'll hear the room respond in real time.

## Muting a room

To silence a room without moving the slider, tap the **speaker icon** next to the slider. Tap it again to unmute and restore the previous volume level.

## Setting a max volume limit

You can place a ceiling on how loud a room will go — useful when you want to make sure a room never blasts past a safe level, no matter who's adjusting it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the room you want to limit">
    Tap the room tab at the bottom of the screen, or select it from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold the volume bar">
    Press and hold anywhere on the volume bar. A control appears that lets you set the maximum volume level for that room.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag to your preferred ceiling">
    Move the limit to the level you want. The room will not go above this value, even if you drag the slider all the way to the right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Max volume limit is a per-room setting — great for a bedroom speaker you never want waking the household, or a living room soundbar that shouldn't rattle the walls when a guest picks up the remote.
</Tip>
