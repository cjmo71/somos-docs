The Volume card sits front and centre in Somos InControl — it's the first thing you see when you open a room, and every change you make applies to your Sonos® speaker the moment you make it. No confirmation step, no delay.

## Adjusting the volume

Drag the slider (0–100) or tap the **−** and **+** buttons at either end. The change goes straight to the speaker over your local Wi-Fi as you drag — you'll hear the room respond in real time. The card stays pinned at the top as you scroll down the page, collapsing to a slim bar that shows a percentage bubble while you drag.

Changes made elsewhere — the official Sonos® app, your TV remote — show up in the slider within a couple of seconds.

## Muting a room

Tap the **speaker icon** on the left of the volume card. The icon changes to a slashed speaker and the room mutes; tap it again and the volume returns to the level it was at. Sliding the volume all the way to **0** also mutes the speaker (this is what the official Sonos® app does — your speaker's status light turns green while muted), and sliding back up restores sound.

## Pinch for volume (off by default)

Menu → **System settings** → **Pinch for volume**. With it on, two fingers anywhere on a room page or the music page change that room's volume — spread them apart to turn it up, pinch them together to turn it down — and a **Volume N%** pill floats at the top while you pinch. The slider still works exactly as before; this is an extra, and it stays off until you switch it on. See [Gestures](/features/gestures).

## Volume from the Apple Watch

Open a room in the [Watch app](/features/apple-watch) and turn the Digital Crown; the number on the screen follows. **Mute** sits beside the number — tap it to mute the room, tap **Unmute** to bring the sound back.

## Volume when rooms are grouped

When a room is grouped with others, every room in the group shows a **Group** slider under its own, on the room page and the music page. It is the group's own volume, the same one the Sonos® app shows: drag it from any of the rooms and every speaker in the group moves together, each keeping its place in the mix. Each room keeps its own slider too, so you can still balance them individually. A room joining a group never comes in louder than the room leading it. On the Apple Watch, a grouped room's Crown turns the group's volume. See [Grouping Rooms](/rooms/groups).

<Note>
  With a soundbar in the group, your TV remote changes only the soundbar's volume, not the other rooms'.
</Note>

## Max volume limit — removed

The per-room max volume ceiling was **removed from the app**. If you remember holding a row in the
EQ card to cap a room, that control is gone.

<Tip>
  The developer is asking whether people miss it — the one-tap **"Do you use the Max volume limit feature?"** question is on the [Help us track down bugs](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/questions.html) page of the website. Your answer decides whether it comes back.
</Tip>
