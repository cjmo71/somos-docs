Somos InControl comes with a small Apple Watch app for the moments you do not want to reach for your phone. Pick a room and everything for it is on one screen: what is playing, the volume on the Crown with Mute beside it, the room's three presets, and — when the room is playing music — previous, play, pause, next and your favorites. Its complications add the room's EQ, the sleep timer, favorites and presets to your watch face. The Watch talks to your speakers directly over Wi-Fi, using the rooms your iPhone sent it.

## How it works, step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Somos on your iPhone">
    Install Somos InControl on your iPhone from the App Store. The Watch app is part of it, but it doesn't always land on the watch by itself — even with **Automatic App Install** on in the Watch app — so the next step matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Somos on your watch">
    Open the **Watch** app on your iPhone and go to **Available Apps**. Tap **Install** next to Somos. If you open **Apple Watch** in Somos' menu before that, a card at the top says **Somos isn't on your Apple Watch yet — open the Watch app › Available Apps › Install**, and the add-face buttons wait until it is installed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Somos on your watch once">
    Open Somos on the watch a single time, so the watch has something to show.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep Somos open on your iPhone the first time">
    Your rooms are sent to the Watch the moment the iPhone app starts, in the same order as your room tabs, together with each room's saved presets. Keep Somos open on your iPhone the first time so it can send them. Until then the Watch says **Open Somos on your iPhone to send your rooms here.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rooms appear">
    The first screen lists your rooms. The Watch keeps the list, so it is there the next time even if the iPhone is out of reach. Tap a room to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The room screen">
    A room is one screen, and what it shows depends on what the room is doing. A **TV room** — a soundbar on its TV input — shows **TV** and the audio format it is receiving (**TV · Dolby Atmos (MAT)**, for example; just **TV** while there is no signal). A **music room** shows the album art, the title and the artist. The Watch reads all of it straight from the speaker over your home Wi-Fi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the Crown for volume, tap Mute">
    Turn the Digital Crown to change the room's volume; the number on screen follows. When the room is grouped, the number is the **group's** volume, and the Crown turns the whole group up or down together, each room keeping its place in the mix. The Crown does nothing until the room's own level has been read — the number shows a dash until then — so a turn can never send another room's level to this one. **Mute** sits beside the number: tap it to mute the room, and it reads **Unmute**, lit, while the room is muted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a preset">
    Under the volume are the room's three [sound presets](/features/sound-presets), by name: the three **TV presets** on a TV room, the three **music presets** on a music room. The loaded one is lit. Tap another and the room takes on everything that preset captured — the EQ, and volume, Loudness, Night Sound or Speech Boost when you ticked them — and the same preset lights up on the iPhone's room page and on the Lock Screen card. A room with no saved presets says **Save presets in Somos on your iPhone.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Previous, play, pause, next (music rooms)">
    On a music room, **Previous** goes back a track, the **Play/Pause** button toggles playback and **Next** skips the track. What you do here shows up in the iPhone app's room and music pages, and the other way round. A TV room has no playback buttons — the TV input would ignore them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Favorites (music rooms)">
    Tap **Favorites** for your Sonos® favorites — the list the Sonos® app keeps. Tap one to play it on the room. The list arrives once the iPhone's music page has loaded it; until then it reads **No favorites yet. Open Music in Somos on your iPhone.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only favorites that can be played directly are listed. Sonos® Radio shortcuts such as **Discover Sonos Radio**, **Playlists**, **Sonos Presents** and **Trending Now** are browse folders rather than something to play, so they are left out — if those are the only favorites you have, the Watch list stays empty.
</Note>

## TV room or music room?

The Watch decides the same way the iPhone app does: by what the speaker is really doing. A soundbar playing its TV input — or sitting idle on it, TV off — is a TV room and shows the TV presets, Mute and the format, nothing else. Anything else is a music room, with playback, the music presets and favorites. Switch the soundbar to music on the iPhone and the Watch screen follows within a few seconds.

## On your watch face

Somos has complications for round, corner and wide slots: the Somos logo, **Room**, **Now Playing**, **Volume**, **EQ Bars**, **EQ LEDs**, **Sleep Timer**, **Favorites**, **Presets**, **Play/Pause** and **Next Room**. Each tap lands on its own screen: an EQ complication opens the room's **EQ** screen, Sleep Timer opens the timer choices, Favorites opens your favorites, and Presets loads the room's next preset. The quickest start is the ready-made face: once Somos is installed on your watch, open the menu on your iPhone, tap **Apple Watch** under **App**, then **Add Infograph face**. See [Watch Face](/features/watch-face) for every complication and how to add it.

## The EQ and sleep timer screens

- **EQ** — opened from an EQ complication. It shows the loaded preset's name, its EQ as bars and its values in words, with the room's preset buttons underneath to load another. With no preset loaded it says **No preset loaded. Pick one below.**
- **Sleep timer** — opened from the Sleep Timer complication. Tap **Off**, **15 min**, **30 min**, **45 min**, **60 min** or **90 min**. While a timer runs, the time left sits at the top. See [Sleep Timer](/features/sleep-timer).

## The Rooms button

A room opened from a complication is the top screen, so it has no back arrow. Press the Digital Crown to go straight back to your watch face, or tap **Rooms** in the top-left corner to see all your rooms.

## The card in the Smart Stack

When [Live Activity](/features/live-activity) is on and the iPhone is running iOS 18 or later, the Lock Screen card also appears in the Watch's Smart Stack. It is a read-only glance, not a remote:

- **TV room** — the room name, the volume, and the audio format.
- **Music room** — the room name, the volume, what is playing and, when the service reports one, the format.

Tap the card and the Watch app opens on that room, with the Crown, Mute, presets and playback ready. The card follows the room the iPhone app has open, like the Lock Screen card does.

## What it cannot do

- **Save or rename presets, or move a slider** — presets are saved and named on the iPhone. The Watch loads them; it has no Bass, Treble, Night Sound or Speech Boost controls of its own.
- **Browsing music** — there is no browse or search. It plays what you already saved as a Sonos® favorite.
- **Press a button from the watch face** — a complication opens the Watch app first; Apple only lets widgets press buttons directly on iPhone and iPad.
- **Grouping** — rooms cannot be grouped or ungrouped from the Watch. On a grouped room, the Crown turns the group's volume.
- **Play, pause or skip on a TV room** — the buttons are not shown there, because a TV input ignores them.
- **Away from home Wi-Fi** — the Watch needs to reach your speakers. If it cannot, the room screen says **Can't reach your Sonos. Join your home Wi-Fi or bring your iPhone.** A room that has dropped off the system says so too — check it has power.
