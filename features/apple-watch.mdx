Somos InControl comes with a small Apple Watch app for the moments you do not want to reach for your phone. Pick a room and everything for it is on one screen: what is playing, the volume as a line the Crown follows with Mute beside it, the room's three presets, and — when the room is playing music — previous, play, pause, next and your favorites. Its complications add the room's EQ, the sleep timer, favourites and presets to your watch face. The Watch talks to your speakers directly over Wi-Fi, using the rooms your iPhone sent it.

## How it works, step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install from TestFlight">
    Install Somos InControl from TestFlight on your iPhone. The Watch app comes along with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Watch app on your iPhone">
    If Somos does not appear on the Watch, open the **Watch** app on your iPhone, go to **My Watch**, and tap **Install** next to Somos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Somos on the iPhone once">
    Your rooms are sent to the Watch the moment the iPhone app starts, in the same order as your bottom bar, together with each room's saved presets. Until then the Watch says **Open Somos on your iPhone to send your rooms here.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rooms appear">
    The first screen lists your rooms, in the same order as the phone's room bar, and a live group is one row. Saved groups are listed under **Favorite Groups**, and an **Alarms** row sits below the rooms. The Watch keeps the list, so it is there the next time even if the iPhone is out of reach. Tap a room to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The room screen">
    A room is one screen, and what it shows depends on what the room is doing. A **TV room** — a soundbar on its TV input — shows **TV** and the audio format it is receiving (**TV · Dolby Atmos (MAT)**, for example; just **TV** while there is no signal). A **music room** shows the album art, the title and the artist. The Watch reads all of it straight from the speaker over your home Wi-Fi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the Crown for volume, tap Mute">
    The volume is a line: the number, then the level in blue on a grey track. A **green box** around it means the Crown is the volume, and the room screen opens that way. Turn the Digital Crown to change the room's volume; the number and the line follow. Scroll the screen with a finger, or tap anywhere else on it, and the box goes: now the Crown scrolls the screen. Tap the volume line, from anywhere down the screen, and the box comes back with the Crown on the volume again. When the room is grouped, the number is the **group's** volume, and the Crown turns the whole group up or down together, each room keeping its place in the mix. The Crown does nothing until the room's own level has been read — the number shows a dash until then — so a turn can never send another room's level to this one. The **speaker button** beside the volume mutes the room; it shows a slashed speaker, lit, while the room is muted. Tap it again to unmute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a preset">
    Under the volume are the room's three [sound presets](/features/sound-presets), by name: the three **TV presets** on a TV room, the three **music presets** on a music room. The loaded one is lit. Tap another and the room takes on everything that preset captured — the EQ, and volume, Loudness, Night Sound or Speech Boost when you ticked them — and the same preset lights up on the iPhone's room page and on the Lock Screen card. A room with no saved presets says **Save presets in Somos on your iPhone.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Previous, play, pause, next (music rooms)">
    On a music room, **Previous** goes back a track, the **Play/Pause** button toggles playback and **Next** skips the track. What you do here shows up in the iPhone app's room and music pages, and the other way round. A TV room has no playback buttons — the TV input would ignore them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Queue, Shuffle and Repeat (music rooms)">
    The room's music screen also has **Shuffle** and **Repeat** beside previous, play and next, and a **Queue** page that lists what is coming up. These are the phone's buttons, on the wrist; a room that is on TV has none of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Favourites (music rooms)">
    Tap **Favourites** for your Sonos® favorites — the list the Sonos® app keeps. Tap one to play it on the room. The list arrives once the iPhone's music page has loaded it; until then it reads **No favourites yet. Open Music in Somos on your iPhone.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only favorites that can be played directly are listed. Sonos® Radio shortcuts such as **Discover Sonos Radio**, **Playlists**, **Sonos Presents** and **Trending Now** are browse folders rather than something to play, so they are left out — if those are the only favorites you have, the Watch list stays empty.
</Note>

## Group, EQ and the room's other page

At the bottom of a room's screen are **Group** and **EQ** buttons, and on a soundbar a button that switches the bar between TV and music; the room's screen follows. Each room has a small **Music** page or **Home Theater** page, depending on what it is playing.

- **Group** — the phone's hold menu on the wrist. **Group with…** lists the other rooms: tick the ones to add and tap **Group**. **Ungroup** splits the group. The phone does the regrouping.
- **EQ** — the room's bars, Loudness and **Sub**, **Surrounds**, Trueplay, **Status Light** and **Touch Controls** switches. A change goes to the speaker and the phone shows it too.
- **Home Theater page** — on a soundbar, Night Sound and **Speech Boost** are one tap away.
- **Presets you can save from the wrist** — under the room's presets, **Save the sound now** puts the room's current sound into an empty preset slot. A slot that already holds a preset is never offered. The preset is on your iPhone too.

## Alarms on the Watch

The **Alarms** row lists your Sonos® alarms with a switch for each. You can switch an alarm on or off; the list is still there after you relaunch the phone app. Alarms are made and edited in Somos on your iPhone. See [Alarms](/features/alarms).

## TV room or music room?

The Watch decides the same way the iPhone app does: by what the speaker is really doing. A soundbar playing its TV input — or sitting idle on it, TV off — is a TV room and shows the TV presets, Mute and the format, nothing else. Anything else is a music room, with playback, the music presets and favorites. Switch the soundbar to music on the iPhone and the Watch screen follows within a few seconds.

## On your watch face

Somos has complications for round, corner and wide slots: the Somos logo, the **Rooms** button (it reads **ROOM**), **Room**, **Now Playing**, **EQ Bars**, **EQ LEDs**, **Sleep Timer**, **Favorites**, **Presets**, and one-tap **Mute**, **Night Sound**, **Speech Boost** and **TV / Music**. Each tap lands on its own screen: the Rooms button opens your rooms, an EQ complication opens the room's **EQ** screen, Sleep Timer opens the timer choices, Favorites opens your favorites, and Presets opens the presets. The one-tap complications act right from the face on watchOS 11 and later. The quickest start is the ready-made face: on your iPhone, open the menu, tap **Apple Watch**, then **Add Infograph face**. See [Watch Face](/features/watch-face) for every complication and how to add it.

## The EQ and sleep timer screens

- **EQ** — opened from an EQ complication. It shows the loaded preset's name, its EQ as bars and its values in words, with the room's preset buttons underneath to load another. With no preset loaded it says **No preset loaded. Pick one below.**
- **Sleep timer** — opened from the Sleep Timer complication. Tap **Off**, **15 min**, **30 min**, **45 min**, **60 min** or **90 min**. While a timer runs, the time left sits at the top. See [Sleep Timer](/features/sleep-timer).

## The Rooms button

A room opened from a complication is the top screen, so it has no back arrow. Press the Digital Crown to go straight back to your watch face, or tap **Rooms** in the top-left corner to see all your rooms.

## The card in the Smart Stack

When [Live Activity](/features/live-activity) is on and the iPhone is running iOS 18 or later, the Lock Screen card also appears in the Watch's Smart Stack, on the same navy as the phone's card. It is a read-only glance, not a remote: three lines and a volume ring.

- **TV room** — the room name, **TV**, and the audio format, with the volume on the ring.
- **Music room** — the room name, the song and artist, and, when the service reports one, the format, with the volume on the ring and the album art.

Tap the card and the Watch app opens on that room, with the Crown, Mute, presets and playback ready. The card follows the room the iPhone app has open, like the Lock Screen card does.

## What it cannot do

- **Name presets or move the EQ sliders** — presets are named on the iPhone. The Watch loads them, and can save the room's current sound into an empty slot.
- **Browsing music** — there is no browse or search. It plays what you already saved as a Sonos® favorite.
- **Press most buttons from the watch face** — only Mute, Night Sound, Speech Boost and TV / Music act right from the face (watchOS 11 and later); every other complication opens the Watch app first.
- **Save a group** — saving a group stays a phone job. The Watch can group and ungroup rooms. On a grouped room, the Crown turns the group's volume.
- **Play, pause or skip on a TV room** — the buttons are not shown there, because a TV input ignores them.
- **Away from home Wi-Fi** — the Watch needs to reach your speakers. If it cannot, the room screen says **Can't reach your Sonos. Join your home Wi-Fi or bring your iPhone.** A room that has dropped off the system says so too — check it has power.
