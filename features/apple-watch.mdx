Somos InControl comes with a small Apple Watch app for the moments you do not want to reach for your phone: pick a room, play or pause, skip, turn the Crown for volume, or start a favorite. The Watch talks to your speakers directly over Wi-Fi, using the rooms your iPhone sent it.

## Installing it

Install Somos InControl from TestFlight on your iPhone and the Watch app comes along with it. If it does not appear on the Watch, open the **Watch** app on your iPhone and look under **Available Apps**. Then open Somos on the iPhone once: your rooms are sent to the Watch, in the same order as your bottom bar.

## What it does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Rooms" icon="house">
    The first screen lists your rooms. Tap one to open it. Until the iPhone has sent them, the screen says so: **Open Somos on your iPhone to send your rooms here.**
  </Card>

  <Card title="Play, pause, next" icon="play">
    A room shows what is playing — title and artist — with **Play/Pause** and **Next** buttons.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Crown = volume" icon="circle-dot">
    Turn the Digital Crown to change the room's volume; the number on screen follows. The Crown does nothing until the room's own level has been read — it shows a dash until then — so a turn can never send another room's level to this one.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Favourites" icon="heart">
    Tap **Favourites** for your Sonos® favorites — the list the Sonos® app keeps. Tap one to play it on the room. The list arrives once the iPhone's music page has loaded it; until then it reads **No favourites yet. Open Music in Somos on your iPhone.**
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  The Watch needs to reach your speakers. If it cannot, the room screen says **Can't reach your Sonos. Join your home Wi-Fi or bring your iPhone.** A room that has dropped off the system says so too — check it has power.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The Lock Screen [Live Activity](/features/live-activity) also mirrors to a paired Watch on iOS 18 and later as a read-only glance card in the Smart Stack.
</Tip>
