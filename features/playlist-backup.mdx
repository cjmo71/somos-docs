Your Somos playlists are yours, so Somos looks after them. Three things work together, and none of them needs an account.

## A backup copy on the phone

Somos keeps a backup copy of your playlists on the phone itself, with no setting to turn on.

## A warning before a big loss

If some playlists exist only on this iPhone, the **Playlists** page tells you how many, for example **2 playlists are only on this iPhone. Deleting Somos would lose them.** A playlist counts as safe once a copy of it is in iCloud settings sync or in an iCloud Drive backup made since its last change. See [iCloud Sync](/features/icloud-sync).

## A backup to iCloud Drive (optional)

On the **Playlists** page, switch on **Back up playlists to iCloud Drive**. The line under the switch shows **Last backup** with the date, or **No backup yet**. If iCloud Drive is off for Somos, the line says **Turn on iCloud Drive for Somos in Settings to use this.** Turn it on in iOS Settings first.

The backup is optional. Leave it off and your playlists still sync through iCloud as before.

See [Favorites & Queue](/features/favorites-and-queue) for how playlists are made.
