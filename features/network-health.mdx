When a room is slow to respond or drops out, **Network health** shows which speaker is the weak link. It sits in the menu, under **Submit diagnostics**, in its own box.

{/* screenshot: IP-10 (iPad: PD-15) */}

## What the page shows

The check runs when the page opens. You get one row per speaker:

- **The room and the model.**
- **How it is connected.** **Wired**, or **Wi-Fi** with the band when the speaker reports it: **2.4 GHz**, **5 GHz** or **6 GHz**.
- **How fast it answers**, in milliseconds.
- **A verdict.** **Good**, **Check** or **Problem**.

A speaker that answers in under 100 ms is fast. From 100 ms up to 400 ms it is slow and reads **Check**. A speaker that does not answer, answers slower than 400 ms, or was missed by the latest scan reads **Problem**.

Under a row that needs attention, a short line says what to try, such as moving the speaker closer to the router or to 5 GHz, or checking the router or mesh point nearest it. A speaker on 2.4 GHz that is still answering fast keeps its green verdict, with a note.

Under the rows, a **summary** in plain words says how many speakers answer fast and what to look at for the rest.

## Refresh and Share

- **Refresh** checks again.
- **Share** sends the result as text or as a PDF, through the iOS share sheet.

<Note>
  The check talks only to your speakers on your own Wi-Fi. Nothing is sent to Somos unless you share it yourself.
</Note>
