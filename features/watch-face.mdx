Somos InControl puts your room one tap from the watch face. The quickest start is a ready-made face with the Somos complications already on it. You can also add any Somos complication to a face you already use.

## Add the Somos watch face

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu on your iPhone">
    Tap the menu button in the top-left corner, then **App → Apple Watch**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add Infograph face">
    **Add Infograph face** is there for every Apple Watch. On an Apple Watch Ultra you also get **Add Modular Ultra face**, a face made for the Ultra.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to My Faces">
    iOS offers to add the face to **My Faces**. Confirm, and it is on your watch with the Somos complications in place.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Before your watch has told Somos which model it is, both buttons show, each labelled with the watches it is for. The buttons are only on an iPhone, because that is where an Apple Watch pairs. If no watch is paired, Somos says **No Apple Watch is paired with this iPhone.**

<Note>
  Premade watch faces are coming in a build soon.
</Note>

## The complications

Each complication is made for one shape of slot: round, corner or wide. The face's editor lists the ones that fit the slot you picked.

| Complication | Slots | What it shows | Tap it and… |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Somos InControl** | Corner | The Somos logo. | Somos opens. |
| **Rooms** (the Room button) | Round, corner | The word **ROOM**, as big as the slot allows. | Your rooms, groups and favorite groups open. Tap one to pick it. |
| **Room** | Wide | The room's name, what is playing — the song and artist, or **TV** and its sound — and the volume. The room line is the name alone, with no symbol. | Now Playing opens for that room. |
| **Now Playing** | Round, corner, inline | Round: the room's volume on a ring, blue to red. Corner: the volume with a curved arc. Inline: what the room is playing. | Now Playing opens. The Crown is the volume. |
| **EQ Bars** | Round, corner, wide, inline | The loaded preset's EQ as bars. In a corner the bars follow the curve of the edge. The wide one shows bold bars for Sub, bass, treble and height, with the preset name. | The room's EQ screen opens. |
| **EQ LEDs** | Round, corner, wide | The loaded preset's EQ as a small cluster of LEDs. The wide one is an LED spectrum; the corner one curves the settings beside it. | The room's EQ screen opens. |
| **Sleep Timer** | Round, corner, wide, inline | The time left on the room's sleep timer, or **Off**. | The sleep timer choices open. See [Sleep Timer](/features/sleep-timer). |
| **Favorites** | Round, corner, wide, inline | A red heart and your Sonos® favorites. | Your favorites list opens. Tap one to play it. |
| **Presets** | Round, corner, wide, inline | The room's presets for what it is doing: the TV presets on TV, the music presets on music, with the loaded one. | The presets open. Tap one to load it. |
| **Mute** | Round, corner | Mute for the room. It wears a filled disc only while the room is muted. | The room mutes or unmutes, right from the face. |
| **Night Sound** | Round, corner | Night Sound for a home theater room, with a filled disc only while it is on. | Night Sound turns on or off. |
| **Speech Boost** | Round, corner | Speech Boost for a home theater room, with a filled disc only while it is on. | Speech Boost turns on or off. On an Arc Ultra or Beam Ultra, which have Speech Boost levels, the tap opens the level row instead. |
| **TV / Music** | Round, corner | Which source the home theater room is on. | The room switches between TV and music. |

<Note>
  The EQ complications draw the loaded preset, not the music. They are a picture of the settings, not live audio. Each bar has a small starting height, so the bars stand at different heights even on a flat EQ. Positive values raise them above that.
</Note>

**Grouped rooms.** When the room is grouped, the volume on the Room, Volume and Now Playing complications is the group's volume.

**No room yet.** Until your iPhone has sent your rooms, a complication shows the Somos icon and **Open Somos**. The volume shows a dash until the watch has read the room's level.

<Note>
  **Mute**, **Night Sound**, **Speech Boost** and **TV / Music** act right from the face on watchOS 11 and later. On an older watch, on a room that is locked, or on a room the switch does not apply to (Night Sound on a music room, for example), the tap opens the Watch app where that control is. Every other Somos complication opens the Watch app on its screen.
</Note>

## Getting back to your rooms

A room opened from a complication is the top screen, with no back arrow. Press the Digital Crown to go straight back to your watch face. To see all your rooms instead, tap **Rooms** in the top-left corner.

## Put a Somos complication on any face

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Somos on your iPhone">
    This sends your rooms and their presets to the Watch. See [Apple Watch](/features/apple-watch) if the Watch app isn't installed yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Somos on the Watch">
    Open it once, so the watch has a room to show on the face.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your watch face">
    Touch and hold the watch face, then tap **Edit**. Swipe left until you reach **Complications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a slot and a Somos complication">
    Tap the slot you want. Turn the Digital Crown to **Somos** and choose a complication from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press the Digital Crown to save, then tap the face to use it. You can also edit faces in the **Watch** app on your iPhone, under **My Faces**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The older white Somos complications from build 37 are gone. If a slot on your face went blank, pick a Somos complication for it again.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  The face keeps up on its own. The complications refresh when the Watch app changes room, loads a preset, changes the volume or sets the sleep timer, and when your iPhone sends new rooms. If the face looks out of date, open Somos on the Watch once.
</Tip>
