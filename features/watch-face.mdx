Somos InControl has five complications, so your room is one tap from the watch face. Each one comes in every shape a face can offer: round, corner, the wide rectangle and the single line of text. Any Somos complication can go in any slot.

## The five complications

| Complication | What it shows | Tap it and… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Somos InControl** (the icon) | The Somos mark | The Watch app opens. |
| **Room** | The room you last used, its loaded preset and its volume. Round, it shows the volume as a ring. | The Watch app opens on that room. |
| **EQ** | The loaded preset's bass, treble, Sub and height as bars above and below a centre line, like a graphic EQ. As one line of text, it reads **Bass +4 · Treble −2**. | The Watch app opens on that room. |
| **Play / Pause** | The play/pause symbol and the room's name. | The Watch app opens on that room, then plays or pauses it. |
| **Next Room** | The next room in your list, for example **Next: Kitchen**. | The Watch app opens on that room. Every Somos complication then moves to it, and Next Room moves on to the room after. |

<Note>
  A watch face complication can't press a button by itself. Apple only allows that on iPhone and iPad. So every Somos complication opens the Watch app first. **Play / Pause** acts a moment later, once the app has read the room. On a TV room it just opens the room, because a TV input has nothing to play or pause.
</Note>

The complications follow the room the Watch app last showed. Until your iPhone has sent your rooms, each one shows the Somos mark or **Open Somos**. The volume shows a dash until the watch has read the room's level. The EQ says **No preset loaded** when the room isn't on a preset, because its bars are the loaded preset's values. Next Room needs at least two rooms.

## Which slot for which complication

- **Big rectangular slots** suit **EQ** and **Room**. On **Modular Ultra**, put **EQ** in the centre: the rectangle there shows the bars with their labels.
- **Round sub-dials** suit **Room** (the volume ring), **Play / Pause** and **Next Room**.
- **Corners** suit **Play / Pause** and **Next Room**. The room name curves along the edge.
- **Wayfinder** has no centre complication slot. Use its sub-dials and corners.

## Put Somos on your watch face

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Somos on your iPhone">
    This sends your rooms and their presets to the Watch. See [Apple Watch](/features/apple-watch) if the Watch app isn't installed yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Somos on the Watch">
    Open it once, so the watch has a room to show on the face.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your watch face">
    Touch and hold the watch face, then tap **Edit**. Swipe left until you reach **Complications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a slot and a Somos complication">
    Tap the slot you want. Turn the Digital Crown to **Somos** and choose **Somos InControl** (the icon), **Room**, **EQ**, **Play / Pause** or **Next Room**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press the Digital Crown to save, then tap the face to use it. You can also edit faces in the **Watch** app on your iPhone, under **My Faces**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The face keeps up on its own. The complications refresh when the Watch app changes room, loads a preset or changes the volume, and when your iPhone sends new rooms. </Tip>
