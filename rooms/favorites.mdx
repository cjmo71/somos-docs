Somos InControl's favorites system puts your most-used rooms right at your fingertips. Instead of opening the menu every time you want to switch speakers, you can reach up to four rooms directly from the tab bar at the bottom of the screen. The favorites aren't a separate list you have to maintain — they're driven automatically by the order of rooms in your menu, so one reorder sets everything.

## How favorites work

The **bottom tab bar** displays up to **4 favorite rooms** as tappable tabs. These tabs are always the top 4 rooms in your menu list — move a room up in the menu and it climbs toward the tab bar; move it down and it falls away from it.

<Tip>
  Put the rooms you reach for most — your living room, home office, bedroom — at the very top of the menu list. They'll lock into the bottom tabs and be one tap away from anywhere in the app.
</Tip>

## Reordering rooms and setting your favorites

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Tap the menu button in the top-left corner of the screen. The menu lists every Sonos® room discovered on your Wi-Fi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold and drag a room">
    Press and hold any room in the list until it lifts, then drag it to a new position. Release to drop it in place. Rooms stack in order from top to bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange your top 4">
    Move your four most-used rooms into the top four slots of the list. As soon as you reorder, the bottom tab bar updates to reflect the new top 4.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the menu">
    Tap outside the menu or swipe to dismiss it. Your new tab order is saved and persists the next time you open the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching rooms

You can switch to any room in two ways:

- **Bottom tab bar** — tap any of the up to 4 favorite tabs to jump to that room instantly.
- **Menu** — tap the menu button in the top-left, then tap any room in the list. This gives you access to all rooms, including ones beyond the top 4.

<Note>
  If you have fewer than 4 rooms on your network, the tab bar simply shows all of your rooms — there's no minimum number required.
</Note>
