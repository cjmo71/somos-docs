Somos InControl's bottom bar puts your most-used rooms right at your fingertips. Instead of opening the menu every time you want to switch speakers, you reach up to four rooms directly from the tabs at the bottom of the screen. The tabs aren't a separate list you maintain — they're driven by the order of rooms in your menu, so one reorder sets everything.

## Hold menus

Anything on the bar that can do more than one thing has a **hold menu**: press and hold it and a short menu of choices opens. It works the same everywhere:

| Hold… | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| A room tab | **Reorder**, **Rename** |
| A room on the room wheel | Opens **Rename Room** straight away |
| A saved group (tab or wheel) | **Rename**, **Ungroup** (only while that group is playing together), **Remove** |
| The **Saved Groups** wheel on its title, or the empty Saved Groups page | **Save current group** (when the room is grouped and that group isn't saved yet) |

## Room tabs

The bottom bar shows up to **4 rooms** as tabs — always the top 4 rooms in your menu list. Move a room up the list and it climbs into the bar; move it down and it falls out. If you have 4 rooms or fewer, they're all there.

A small **Hold to reorder or rename** hint sits under the tabs whenever there are two or more rooms. Hold a tab to open its menu:

- **Reorder** — the tabs wiggle and the hint reads **Drag to reorder**. Drag a tab left or right and let go. Tap **Done** at the end of the bar when you're finished.
- **Rename** — opens **Rename Room** with the room's name, up to 12 characters. Renaming changes the room's name in the Sonos® system, so the official app sees it too.

A plain tap still switches room. The same order can be set from the menu:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Tap the menu button in the top-left corner. With four rooms or fewer, they're listed under **Rooms** ("hold to reorder"). With more, tap the **Rooms** row: a pop-up lists your four favourites (the tabs) in a box above the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold and drag a room">
    Press and hold any room until it lifts, then drag it to a new position and release. In the pop-up, drag a room between the boxes to change your four favourites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange your top 4">
    Whatever sits in the top four becomes the bottom tabs the moment you drop it. The order is saved and persists across launches. Signed in to iCloud, the same order (and your saved groups) follows you to your other devices. See [iCloud Sync](/features/icloud-sync).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also rename a room by pressing and holding its name at the top of its page. Looking for the favorites you heart on the music page? Those are on the [Favorites & Queue](/features/favorites-and-queue) page.
</Tip>

## The room wheel

Prefer to spin rather than tap? Open the menu, then **System settings**, and switch on **Room wheel**. It is off by default. The four tabs on the bottom bar become a wheel that holds **every** room, in your menu order. Spin it to pick a room. The rooms above and below the selection show faded, like the iPhone's own pickers. The **Saved Groups** button stays at the right-hand end.

Hold the room in the middle of the wheel to rename it. Reordering stays in the menu, or on the tabs with the wheel off.

The wheel sits in a slim bar, so more of the room page stays on screen. Switch **Room wheel** off to get the tabs back. Your room order is the same either way.

## Saved groups (second page of the bar)

The button at the right-hand end of the bar flips it between **Rooms** and **Saved Groups**. Saved groups are combinations of rooms you've grouped together and saved — "Downstairs", "Whole House" — up to **4** of them. With the tabs, they show as tabs. With the **Room wheel** on, they're on a **Saved Groups** wheel instead, the room wheel's twin.

- **Save one** — group the rooms you want (see [Grouping Rooms](/rooms/groups)), then tap **Save as favourite** at the bottom of the Group Rooms sheet. With the wheel on, you can also hold the **Saved Groups** wheel on its title and tap **Save current group**, then name it (up to 12 characters).
- **Recall one** — tap its tab, or spin the wheel to it. On the wheel, the group is built about a second after the wheel comes to rest, so you can spin past groups without each one assembling. The bar stays on Saved Groups. The app rebuilds that group on your speakers: it joins the rooms, and a spinner runs over the bar for a few seconds while the speakers settle. If rooms that aren't part of the saved group are already grouped with it, the app asks whether to keep or drop them rather than guessing.
- **Rename one** — hold it and tap **Rename**.
- **Ungroup** — hold it and tap **Ungroup** to split the speakers back into separate rooms. This only shows while that group is playing together, so you're never stuck grouped.
- **Remove one** — hold it and tap **Remove**. If the group is playing together at the time, its speakers are split back into separate rooms too. You can also open Group Rooms on that group and tap **Remove from favourites**.

Saved groups are part of [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home), like grouping itself.

## Switching rooms

- **Bottom tabs** — tap any of the up to 4 room tabs to jump straight there, or swipe left and right across the room pages.
- **Room wheel** — with the wheel switched on, spin it to any room. See [The room wheel](#the-room-wheel).
- **Menu** — tap the menu button, then any room in the list. This reaches every room, including ones beyond the top 4.
