Somos InControl's bottom bar puts your rooms right at your fingertips. Instead of opening the menu every time you want to switch speakers, you tap a tab at the bottom of the screen. There is nothing to set up: every room gets a tab, and the bar remembers where you were.

## Hold menus

Anything on the bar that can do more than one thing has a **hold menu**: press and hold it and a short menu of choices opens. It works the same everywhere:

| Hold… | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| A room tab | **Reorder**, **Rename** |
| A room on the room wheel | Opens **Rename Room** straight away |
| A saved group (tab or wheel) | **Rename**, **Ungroup** (only while that group is playing together), **Remove** |
| The **Saved Groups** wheel on its title, or the empty Saved Groups page | **Save current group** (when the room is grouped and that group isn't saved yet) |

## Room tabs

The bottom bar shows four rooms at a time, and it swipes: **slide the bar sideways** to reach every other room. With more than four rooms, the tabs loop around endlessly, so you never hit an end. With four rooms or fewer they all fit and the bar stays put.

A tab's label wraps onto up to two lines and shrinks to fit rather than being cut off, so you can tell one room from another. Two small marks show how a room is set up:

- **Two speakers** in place of the single speaker on the tab means the room is a **stereo pair** or a **home theater**.
- **A small link badge**, and a shared color on the tab, means the room is **grouped** with others. Rooms in the same group wear the same color, and the marks update as you group and ungroup. Every room still has its own tab and its own page.

A room your plan doesn't cover shows a lock in place of its speaker, and opens the [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home) page.

A small **Hold to reorder or rename** hint sits under the tabs whenever there are two or more rooms. Hold a tab to open its menu:

- **Reorder** — the tabs wiggle and the hint reads **Drag to reorder**. Drag a tab left or right and let go. Tap **Done** at the end of the bar when you're finished.
- **Rename** — opens **Rename Room** with the room's name, up to 12 characters. Renaming changes the room's name in the Sonos® system, so the official app sees it too.

A plain tap still switches room. The tab order is the only room order in the app — the menu's **Rooms** pop-up lists the same rooms and does not reorder them. The order is saved and persists across launches. Signed in to iCloud, the same order (and your saved groups) follows you to your other devices. See [iCloud Sync](/features/icloud-sync).

## Coming back to your room

The tabs and the wheel stay on the room you were in. Switching a room between **TV** and **Music**, closing the app and opening it again — you land on the last room you used, and the bar shows it first.

## The room wheel

Prefer to spin rather than tap? Open the menu, then **Somos settings** (under **App**), and switch on **Room wheel**. It is off by default. The tabs on the bottom bar become a wheel that holds **every** room, in your tab order. Spin it to pick a room; it spins endlessly, with no end to hit. The rooms above and below the selection show faded, like the iPhone's own pickers. Rooms show the same marks as the tabs: two speakers for a stereo pair or home theater, and a link badge in the group's color for rooms grouped together. The **Saved Groups** button stays at the right-hand end.

Hold the room in the middle of the wheel to rename it; a **Hold to rename** hint under the wheel says so. Reordering is on the tabs: switch **Room wheel** off, hold a tab and tap **Reorder**.

The wheel sits in a slim bar, so more of the room page stays on screen. Switch **Room wheel** off to get the tabs back. Your room order is the same either way.

## Saved groups (second page of the bar)

The button at the right-hand end of the bar flips it between **Rooms** and **Saved Groups**. Saved groups are combinations of rooms you've grouped together and saved — "Downstairs", "Whole House" — up to **4** of them. With the tabs, they show as tabs. With the **Room wheel** on, they're on a **Saved Groups** wheel instead, the room wheel's twin. Until you save one, the page says **No favorite groups yet — save one from a room's Group button**.

- **Save one** — group the rooms you want (see [Grouping Rooms](/rooms/groups)), then tap **Save as favorite** at the bottom of the Group Rooms sheet. With the wheel on, you can also hold the **Saved Groups** wheel on its title and tap **Save current group**, then name it (up to 12 characters).
- **Recall one** — tap its tab, or spin the wheel to it. On the wheel, the group is built about a second after the wheel comes to rest, so you can spin past groups without each one assembling. The bar stays on Saved Groups. The app rebuilds that group on your speakers: it joins the rooms, and a spinner runs over the bar for a few seconds while the speakers settle. If rooms that aren't part of the saved group are already grouped with it, the app asks whether to keep or drop them rather than guessing.
- **Rename one** — hold it and tap **Rename**.
- **Ungroup** — hold it and tap **Ungroup** to split the speakers back into separate rooms. This only shows while that group is playing together, so you're never stuck grouped.
- **Remove one** — hold it and tap **Remove**. If the group is playing together at the time, its speakers are split back into separate rooms too. You can also open Group Rooms on that group and tap **Remove from favorites**.

Saved groups are part of [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home), like grouping itself.

## Switching rooms

- **Bottom tabs** — tap any room tab to jump straight there, slide the bar sideways to reach the rest, or swipe left and right across the room pages.
- **Room wheel** — with the wheel switched on, spin it to any room. See [The room wheel](#the-room-wheel).
- **Menu** — tap the menu button, then **See all** under **Rooms** and any room in the pop-up.
