Grouped rooms play the same audio in sync. Somos InControl lets you build and break groups from any room page, keeps every room's volume within reach while grouped, and lets you save a group you use often so a single tap rebuilds it.

<Note>
  Grouping and saved groups are part of [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home), the one-time purchase that covers every room beyond your free one.
</Note>

## Grouping and ungrouping

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the room you want as the anchor">
    Whatever this room is playing is what the group will play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the group button">
    It's in the top-right of the room page. The **Group Rooms** sheet lists every other room on the system with a switch. On a system with only one room there is nothing to group with, so the button isn't shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn rooms on or off">
    Switch a room **on** to add it to this room's group; switch it **off** to make it standalone again. Changes apply immediately. Tap **Done** when you're finished.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Two rooms that share the same name can't be told apart by Sonos® when grouping. If the sheet points this out, rename one of them first — press and hold the room name at the top of its page.
</Note>

## Volume in a group

While a room is grouped, its status line reads **Grouped with …**. The room that started the group shows a single **Group** slider that moves every speaker in the group together, and each room keeps its own slider on its own page, so you can still balance them to taste. Presets, EQ and the other controls on the page still apply to that room only.

## Group Audio Delay

When a soundbar is grouped with other rooms, a **Group Audio Delay** stepper appears in its EQ card. It steps the delay applied to the *other* rooms so their audio lines up with the bar instead of echoing — the same setting the Sonos® app keeps under System settings. It's hidden on an ungrouped room because it has no effect there.

## Saving a group as a favourite

At the bottom of the Group Rooms sheet, tap **Save as favourite** to keep this combination. Saved groups live on the second page of the bottom bar (up to 4 slots) and are rebuilt with one tap. See [Favorites](/rooms/favorites#saved-groups-second-page-of-the-bar).
