In Somos InControl, a "room" maps directly to a Sonos® room on your home network. Every room you've set up in your Sonos® system — whether it's a full home theater with a soundbar and surrounds, a stereo pair, or a single standalone speaker — appears in the app as its own room. What you can control in each room depends on the hardware in it: all rooms share a common set of controls, and certain room types unlock additional ones.

<Note>
  Somos InControl discovers every Sonos® room on your Wi-Fi automatically each time the app launches. You don't need to add or configure rooms — they appear as soon as the app finds them, usually within a couple of seconds.
</Note>

## What each room type supports

Every room type gets the full set of core audio controls. Soundbar rooms and rooms with a bonded subwoofer each gain an additional layer of controls on top of that foundation.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="All Rooms" icon="speaker">
    Every room on your network — home theaters, stereo pairs, and single speakers — gets these controls:

    - Volume slider
    - Bass and treble EQ
    - Loudness toggle
    - Sound presets
    - Trueplay status and toggle
    - Music
  </Card>
  <Card title="Soundbar Rooms" icon="tv">
    Rooms built around a soundbar (home theater rooms) include everything above, plus the home-theater-exclusive controls:

    - Height channel level
    - Speech Boost toggle
    - Night Sound toggle
    - TV source switch
    - Audio-format badge
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rooms with a Subwoofer" icon="waveform">
    Any room that has a subwoofer bonded — whether it's attached to a soundbar or a non-soundbar speaker — adds one extra control on top of its room type:

    - Subwoofer level slider
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Switching rooms

The bar at the bottom of the screen shows up to **4 favorite rooms** as tabs. Tap any tab to switch to that room instantly. To see and access all of your rooms, tap the menu button in the top-left corner — every room on your network appears there. Tap any room in the menu to switch to it.

To control which rooms appear as bottom tabs, reorder the list in the menu. Hold and drag any room to a new position — whatever sits in the top 4 slots becomes the four bottom tabs.

## Room types at a glance

Somos InControl handles all three Sonos® room configurations without any setup on your part:

- **Home theater rooms** — a soundbar, optionally with surround speakers and/or a subwoofer. Gets the full home theater control set.
- **Stereo pairs** — two matching speakers grouped together. Gets core controls, plus a subwoofer slider if one is bonded.
- **Single speakers** — one standalone speaker. Gets core controls, plus a subwoofer slider if one is bonded.
