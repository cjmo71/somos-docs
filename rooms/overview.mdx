In Somos InControl, a "room" maps directly to a Sonos® room on your home network. Every room in your Sonos® system — a home theater with a soundbar and surrounds, a stereo pair, a single standalone speaker, a portable Move or Roam — gets its own page. What you can control in each room depends on the hardware in it: all rooms share a common set of controls, and certain room types unlock more.

<Note>
  Somos InControl discovers every Sonos® room on your Wi-Fi automatically each time the app launches. You don't need to add or configure rooms — they appear as soon as the app finds them, usually within a few seconds.
</Note>

## Anatomy of a room page

From top to bottom:

1. **Room name** — press and hold it to rename the room. The new name applies everywhere, including the Sonos® app.
2. **Status line** — connection state and the model ("Era 100 · Stereo Pair", "Move 2 · 87% ⚡" for a portable with its battery level). The **group** button in the top-right opens [Group Rooms](/rooms/groups); on a system with only one room there is nothing to group with, so the button isn't shown.
3. **Source row** (soundbars) — TV / Music pills and the audio-format badge; while music plays, the format the service reports for the track scrolls there instead. On other rooms, the **Music** button opens the [music page](/features/music). A music speaker in a group a soundbar leads shows the soundbar's TV row instead. While a [sleep timer](/features/sleep-timer) runs, a moon and the minutes left (**☾ 23m**) sit on this row.
4. **Volume card** — pinned as you scroll. See [Volume](/features/volume). When the room is grouped, a line under it names the group (**Office grouped with Era Right S**) above the **Group** slider.
5. **Sound Presets** — three TV presets. See [Sound Presets](/features/sound-presets).
6. **EQ card** — sliders and toggles. See [EQ & Trueplay](/features/eq-and-trueplay).
7. **Room diagram** — what's bonded to the room; press and hold to set up speakers. See [Room Diagram](/rooms/room-diagram).

On a soundbar with **Music** lit, the page stops at the volume card: presets, EQ, toggles and the diagram step aside while music plays, and the music presets and EQ are on the music page. Tap **TV** and everything is back.

## TV rooms and music rooms

Only a room built around a **soundbar** — Arc, Arc Ultra, Beam, Beam Ultra, Ray, Playbar or Playbase — is a TV room, with the TV / Music source row, the three TV presets and the full EQ card on its page. Everything else is a **music room**: a single speaker, a stereo pair (two Eras, Ones or Fives), an Amp, a portable — with or without a Sub bonded to it. A Sub on its own never turns a room into a TV room.

A music room's page is shorter on purpose. It keeps volume, **Balance** on a stereo pair, the Sub's on/off switch, crossover and phase when a Sub is bonded, the Trueplay, Status Light and Touch Controls switches, and the room diagram (press and hold it to make or separate a pair). The room's sound — its three presets, bass, treble, Sub level and Loudness — is on the [music page](/features/music), because music is all that room plays. Tap **Music** at the top of the page to get there.

## What each room type supports

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="All rooms" icon="speaker">
    - Volume, − / +, mute
    - Bass, treble, Loudness
    - Three music presets on the music page
    - Trueplay, Status Light, Touch Controls
    - Music page (music services, favorites, queue)
    - Grouping, rename, room diagram
  </Card>
  <Card title="Soundbar rooms" icon="tv">
    Everything above, plus:
    - Three TV presets and the EQ card on the room page
    - TV / Music source pills and the audio-format badge
    - Night Sound and Speech Boost (a level slider on the Arc Ultra)
    - Height level on height-capable bars
    - Surround level and Surrounds on/off (with surrounds bonded)
    - Music Level and Ambient / Full on the music page
    - Group Audio Delay (while grouped)
    - Home Theater Setup from the diagram
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stereo pairs" icon="circle-half-stroke">
    Everything in "All rooms", plus a **Balance** slider on the room page, and the option to separate the pair from the diagram's setup sheet. A pair is a music room: its presets and EQ are on the music page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rooms with a Sub" icon="waveform">
    Any room with a bonded Sub — soundbar, stereo pair or single — adds the **Subwoofer** level, an on/off switch, **Sub phase** (Normal / Reversed), and the **Crossover** slider wherever the speaker reports one. A Sub does not make a room a TV room.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Portable speakers" icon="battery-half">
    Move, Move 2 and Roam appear as normal rooms with their battery level (and a ⚡ while charging) in the status line. A portable switched to Bluetooth disappears from the app — and from the Sonos® app — until Bluetooth is turned off again.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rooms that need attention" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    A speaker running older firmware than the rest of the household shows **Needs a firmware update** and its page has no controls until the Sonos® app updates it. A Sub that isn't bonded to anything gets a page explaining where to bond it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Switching rooms

- **Swipe** left or right on a room page to move between rooms.
- **Bottom bar** — the top 4 rooms in your menu order sit here as tabs. Tap to switch instantly; press and hold a tab for **Reorder** or **Rename** — a **Hold to reorder or rename** hint sits under the tabs whenever there are two or more. A button at the end flips the bar to your **Saved Groups**. See [Favorite Rooms](/rooms/favorites).
- **Menu** — tap the menu button in the top-left; your rooms are under **Rooms** (with more than four, tap **Rooms** to open them all in a pop-up). Tap a room to switch to it, hold and drag to reorder. You can also rename a room by pressing and holding its name on the room page.
- **Apple Watch** — the [Watch app](/features/apple-watch) lists the same rooms in the same order.

If you have more than one Sonos® system (an S1 and an S2 household, or two homes), the menu's **System** section lets you switch between them. See [Multiple Systems](/rooms/multiple-systems).
