In Somos InControl, a "room" maps directly to a Sonos® room on your home network. Every room in your Sonos® system — a home theater with a soundbar and surrounds, a stereo pair, a single standalone speaker, a portable Move or Roam — gets its own page. What you can control in each room depends on the hardware in it: all rooms share a common set of controls, and certain room types unlock more.

<Note>
  Somos InControl discovers every Sonos® room on your Wi-Fi automatically each time the app launches. You don't need to add or configure rooms — they appear as soon as the app finds them, usually within a few seconds.
</Note>

## Anatomy of a room page

From top to bottom:

1. **Room name** — press and hold it to rename the room. The new name applies everywhere, including the Sonos® app.
2. **Status line** — connection state and the model ("Era 100 · Stereo Pair", "Move 2 · 87% ⚡" for a portable with its battery level), or "Grouped with …" when the room is in a group. The **group** button in the top-right opens [Group Rooms](/rooms/groups).
3. **Source row** (soundbars) — TV / Music pills and the audio-format badge. On other rooms, the **Music** button opens the [Music sheet](/features/music).
4. **Volume card** — pinned as you scroll. See [Volume](/features/volume).
5. **Sound Presets** — three TV presets. See [Sound Presets](/features/sound-presets).
6. **EQ card** — sliders and toggles. See [EQ & Trueplay](/features/eq-and-trueplay).
7. **Room diagram** — what's bonded to the room; press and hold to set up speakers. See [Room Diagram](/rooms/room-diagram).

Pull down anywhere on the page to force a refresh; the app also resyncs in the background about every 30 seconds.

## What each room type supports

  <Card title="All rooms" icon="speaker">
    - Volume, − / +, mute
    - Bass, treble, Loudness
    - Three TV presets and three music presets
    - Trueplay, Status Light, Touch Controls
    - Music sheet (favorites, playlists, queue)
    - Grouping, rename, room diagram
  </Card>
  <Card title="Soundbar rooms" icon="tv">
    Everything above, plus:
    - TV / Music source pills and the audio-format badge
    - Night Sound and Speech Boost (a level slider on the Arc Ultra)
    - Height level on height-capable bars
    - Surround level and Surrounds on/off (with surrounds bonded)
    - Music Level and Ambient / Full on the Music sheet
    - Group Audio Delay (while grouped)
    - Home Theater Setup from the diagram
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stereo pairs" icon="circle-half-stroke">
    Everything in "All rooms", plus a **Balance** slider, and the option to separate the pair from the diagram's setup sheet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rooms with a Sub" icon="waveform">
    Any room with a bonded Sub — soundbar, stereo pair or single — adds the **Subwoofer** level, an on/off switch, and the **Crossover** slider wherever the speaker reports one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Portable speakers" icon="battery-half">
    Move, Move 2 and Roam appear as normal rooms with their battery level (and a ⚡ while charging) in the status line. A portable switched to Bluetooth disappears from the app — and from the Sonos® app — until Bluetooth is turned off again.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rooms that need attention" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    A speaker running older firmware than the rest of the household shows **Needs a firmware update** and its page has no controls until the Sonos® app updates it. A Sub that isn't bonded to anything gets a page explaining where to bond it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Switching rooms

- **Swipe** left or right on a room page to move between rooms.
- **Bottom bar** — the top 4 rooms in your menu order sit here as tabs. Tap to switch instantly. A button at the end flips the bar to your **Saved Groups**. See [Favorite Rooms](/rooms/favorites).
- **Menu** — tap the menu button in the top-left; every room on the current system is listed. Tap a room to switch to it, hold and drag to reorder, or swipe left on a room to rename it.

If you have more than one Sonos® system (an S1 and an S2 household, or two homes), the menu's **System** section lets you switch between them. See [Multiple Systems](/rooms/multiple-systems).
