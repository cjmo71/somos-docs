Most households have a single Sonos® system, and Somos InControl simply shows its rooms. If you have more than one — an older S1 system alongside an S2 system, or Sonos® at a second address — the app keeps each as its own **system** and lets you switch between them from the menu.

## The Your Sonos page in the menu

Open the menu (top-left) and tap **Your Sonos**. The page shows:

- **One system** — its name and generation (S1 or S2). Press and hold to rename it.
- **Several systems** — one row each with the room count; the active one is ticked. Tap another to switch — the room list and bottom tabs change to that system. Press and hold a row to rename it.

Below that:

- **Add a Home / New S1 or S2 System** — give the system a name (optional, e.g. "Lake House"), make sure your phone is on that system's Wi-Fi, and tap **Scan for System**. Once found, it appears in the switcher.
- **Rescan My Network** — looks again for systems that didn't show up, for example after a router reboot, or when an S1 system was hiding an S2 one. You watch the scan happen.

## Did we find everything?

If discovery finds two systems, or suspects one is missing, the app asks **"Did we find everything?"** once your rooms are on screen. Answer **No, something's missing** to run a rescan. If a whole system never appears, check **Help & Support → Settings Somos needs** in the menu, then send **Submit diagnostics** (also in the menu) to the developer along with the [Speakers Not Found questionnaire](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/speakers-not-found) on the website.

<Note>
  Every system is controlled over your local Wi-Fi. To control a second home you need to be on that home's network — there is no remote access, by design.
</Note>
