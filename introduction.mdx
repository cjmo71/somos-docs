Somos InControl is an iPhone and iPad app that gives you a single, focused screen for controlling your Sonos® speakers. Open it, pick a room, and you're one tap from volume, EQ, sound presets, and home theater controls — with no login, no cloud round-trip, and no ads getting in the way. It's built for people who want to adjust their sound quickly and get back to whatever they were doing.

## Why Somos InControl?

The official Sonos® app has grown larger and slower over time. Somos InControl is the opposite: one screen, the controls you actually reach for, and nothing else in the way. Because it works entirely over your local Wi-Fi — never through a cloud server — every control responds instantly and your activity stays on your network.

## Which Sonos® Hardware Is Supported?

Somos InControl works with most current and recent Sonos® speakers that support local control on your network. If a speaker appears in the official Sonos® app on the same Wi-Fi, it will appear here too.

Every room — regardless of type — gets **volume, bass, treble, loudness, sound presets, and Trueplay**. What changes by room type:

| Room type | Additional controls |
|---|---|
| Soundbar room | Height channel, Speech Boost, Night Sound, TV source, audio-format badge |
| Any room with a bonded subwoofer | Subwoofer level slider |
| Stereo pair or single speaker | Managed via the standard EQ controls above |

If a specific speaker model isn't being discovered, contact the developer through the in-app Support & Feedback form with the model name.
