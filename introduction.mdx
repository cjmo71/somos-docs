Somos InControl is an iPhone and iPad app that gives you a single, focused page for each of your Sonos® rooms. Open it, pick a room, and you're one tap from volume, EQ, sound presets, home theater controls and music — with no login, no cloud round-trip, and no ads getting in the way. It's built for people who want to adjust their sound quickly and get back to whatever they were doing.

## Why Somos InControl?

The official Sonos® app has grown larger and slower over time. Somos InControl is the opposite: one page per room, the controls you actually reach for, and nothing else in the way. Because it works entirely over your local Wi-Fi — never through a cloud server — every control responds instantly and your activity stays on your network.

## Which Sonos® hardware is supported?

Somos InControl works with Sonos® speakers that support local control on your network — S2 and S1 systems, and more than one system at a time. If a speaker appears in the official Sonos® app on the same Wi-Fi, it will appear here too.

Every room gets **volume, mute, bass, treble, loudness, presets, Trueplay, Status Light, Touch Controls and the Music sheet**. What changes by room type:

| Room type | Additional controls |
|---|---|
| Soundbar room | TV / Music source, audio-format badge, Night Sound, Speech Boost, height (height-capable bars), surround level and on/off, Group Audio Delay, home theater setup |
| Stereo pair | Balance slider, separate-pair option |
| Any room with a bonded Sub | Subwoofer level and on/off; crossover on a Sonos Amp |
| Portable (Move, Roam) | Battery level in the status line |

If a specific speaker model isn't being discovered, contact the developer through **Support & Feedback** in the app menu with the model name.
