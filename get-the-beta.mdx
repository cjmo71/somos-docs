Somos InControl is in public beta on iPhone and in a closed test on Android. Pick your phone below. It takes a couple of minutes either way.

## iPhone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install TestFlight">
    Somos InControl is distributed through Apple's TestFlight. If you don't have it already, download **TestFlight** from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the beta">
    Open this link on your device: **[testflight.apple.com/join/2D778AXH](https://testflight.apple.com/join/2D778AXH)**

    Tap **Accept** on the TestFlight page, then tap **Install**. TestFlight tells you when updates are available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Android

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the testers group">
    On your phone, open **[groups.google.com/g/somos-android-testers](https://groups.google.com/g/somos-android-testers)** and tap **Join group**. Use the same Google account the Play Store on your phone uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Become a tester">
    Open **[play.google.com/apps/testing/com.cmoore.somosincontrol](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.cmoore.somosincontrol)** and tap **Become a tester**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install from Google Play">
    Wait a few minutes, then install Somos InControl from Google Play. Updates arrive on their own.
  </Step>
</Steps>
