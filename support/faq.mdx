If you have a question about Somos InControl, you're in the right place. These are the questions users ask most often, covering everything from how the app connects to your speakers to what it does (and doesn't) do with your data. If your question isn't answered here, head to [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting) for step-by-step fixes, or [contact support](/support/contact) directly.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Is this made by Sonos®?">
    No. Somos InControl is an independent, unofficial third-party app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Sonos, Inc. It uses the publicly documented local control protocol to communicate with Sonos® speakers on your Wi-Fi network.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which Sonos® speakers does it work with?">
    Somos InControl works with most current and recent Sonos® speakers that support local control on the network. Every room appears in the app — home theater (soundbar) rooms, stereo pairs, and single speakers. As a general rule: if your speaker shows up in the official Sonos® app on the same Wi-Fi, it should show up here too.

    All rooms get volume, bass, treble, loudness, sound presets, Trueplay status, and music controls. Home-theater-only controls — height, Speech Boost, Night Sound, TV source, and the audio-format badge — appear only in rooms that include a soundbar. A subwoofer slider appears in any room that has a subwoofer bonded.

    If a specific model isn't being discovered, contact support with the model name and the team will investigate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a Sonos® account?">
    No. Somos InControl does not use your Sonos® account and will never ask you to log in. It talks to your speakers directly over Wi-Fi — no credentials, no cloud, no sign-in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does the app ask for Local Network permission?">
    iOS requires any app that discovers and communicates with devices on your local Wi-Fi to request Local Network permission. Somos InControl needs this permission to find your Sonos® speakers. Without it, the app has nothing to control. The permission only lets the app see devices on the Wi-Fi network you're currently connected to — nothing else.

    If you declined the prompt initially, re-enable it in **Settings → Somos InControl → Local Network**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does it work over cellular or when I'm away from home?">
    No. Somos InControl only works on the same local Wi-Fi network as your Sonos® speakers. This is a deliberate design choice — it's what keeps the app fast, private, and free of any account requirement. If you're away from home and need remote control, use the official Sonos® app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I save a preset?">
    Dial in the sound you want, then **hold** a preset button to save your current settings into it. **Tap** a preset to load it, and **hold** it again to rename it. Presets capture your EQ levels along with the Night Sound and Speech Boost toggles.

    While a preset is active, changing any control it captured will deactivate it — a brief toast notification will explain what happened.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I control more than one room?">
    Yes. The tab bar at the bottom of the screen holds up to four favorite rooms — tap a tab to switch between them. The menu (top-left) lists all of your rooms: tap any room to switch to it, or hold and drag to reorder the list. Whatever sits in the top four positions of that list becomes the bottom tabs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why isn't the Dynamic Island or Lock Screen control showing?">
    The Live Activity — the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island volume control with **+** and **−** buttons and the current volume percentage — is off by default. Open the menu and enable **Live Activity** to make it appear.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Trueplay and should I enable it?">
    Trueplay is Sonos®'s room calibration feature. It tunes your speaker's sound to match the acoustics of your room. If your speaker supports Trueplay, turning it on generally improves sound quality. Somos InControl shows the current Trueplay status in every room and lets you toggle it on or off.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is Trueplay unavailable after I added front speakers?">
    Whenever you set up a configuration that Sonos® doesn't officially support — such as separate front speakers — Trueplay becomes unavailable. This is a Sonos® firmware restriction, not something Somos InControl can work around. The trade-off is yours to make: a larger soundstage with your custom speaker configuration, or your original layout with Trueplay enabled.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Somos InControl update my speakers' firmware?">
    No — firmware updates are handled exclusively by the official Sonos® app. What Somos InControl does do: if one of your speakers is running a different firmware version than the others, it tells you so (the speaker appears with a "needs a firmware update" note), giving you a heads-up before configuration issues occur. If all your speakers match each other, Somos stays quiet — it never nags you to update.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use a single speaker as a surround?">
    Yes. The official Sonos® app requires surrounds in matched pairs, but Somos InControl lets you bond a single speaker as a surround for special circumstances. If you install it in the left surround position you'll receive only the left surround channel (and vice versa for the right). This is likely most useful for Era 300 owners, since that speaker has its own Atmos capability.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can Somos InControl send music to non-Sonos AirPlay speakers?">
    Not from inside the app — Apple doesn't allow third-party apps to initiate AirPlay to external speakers. However, AirPlay-capable Sonos® speakers accept AirPlay directly from the iOS AirPlay picker, where they appear alongside any other AirPlay speakers in your home. So the mix-and-match you might be imagining is partly available at the iPhone level, just not from within Somos InControl itself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Somos InControl collect any data about me or my speakers?">
    No. Nothing leaves your device except the local control messages your speakers need to respond to your inputs. There is no analytics, no telemetry, and no account to send data to. See the [Privacy Policy](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/privacy.html) for full details.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
