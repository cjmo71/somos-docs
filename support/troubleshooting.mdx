title: "Somos InControl: Troubleshooting Common App Issues"
sidebarTitle: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Step-by-step fixes for the most common Somos InControl problems — speakers not found, sliders snapping back, presets, Live Activity, home theater setup, and more."
---

This page covers the issues users run into most often and walks you through how to resolve each one. Work through the relevant section below; most problems have a straightforward fix. If none of these steps resolve your issue, the [contact form](/support/contact) goes straight to the developer.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="The app can't find my speakers">
    The most common cause is a network mismatch or a missing permission. Work through these steps in order:

    1. **Check you're on the same Wi-Fi network.** Your iPhone or iPad and your Sonos® speakers must be on the same network — not a guest network or a separate IoT VLAN. Many routers broadcast separate SSIDs for 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands; connect both to the same one.
    2. **Verify Local Network permission.** If you declined the prompt when the app first launched, go to **Settings → Somos InControl → Local Network** and turn it on (the menu's **Open iOS Settings for Somos**, under **System settings**, the box just under Rooms, takes you there). Then tap **Try Again**.
    3. **Check the rest of the list.** Menu → **Help & Support → Settings Somos needs**: UPnP on in the Sonos® app, VPN off, not a guest or IoT network.
    4. **Rescan.** Open the menu and tap **Rescan My Network** under **System**. Discovery also runs fresh on every launch, so force-quitting and reopening works too.
    4. **Test with the official Sonos® app.** If it can't find your speakers either, the problem is the network itself — restart your router and speakers, then test again.

    If your speakers appear in the official app but not in Somos InControl, send **Submit diagnostics** (menu → **Help us track bugs**) to the developer and fill in the [Speakers Not Found questionnaire](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/speakers-not-found.html) on the website.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A speaker or an entire system didn't show up">
    If some rooms are missing — especially with an S1 and an S2 system, or two homes — these are the usual causes:

    - **Same Wi-Fi requirement.** See the section above.
    - **A second system.** Somos InControl keeps each Sonos® system separate. Open the menu: if more than one system is listed under **System**, tap the other one. If it isn't listed, use **Add a Home / New S1 or S2 System** or **Rescan My Network**. See [Multiple Systems](/rooms/multiple-systems).
    - **A quarantined speaker.** A speaker on older firmware than the rest of the household shows **Needs a firmware update** and can't be controlled until the Sonos® app has updated it.

    If speakers are still missing, the [Speakers Not Found questionnaire](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/speakers-not-found.html) is the best next step.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A slider snaps back when I drag it">
    The speaker rejected the value. This usually happens when Trueplay is active for that control, the room is in a group, or the connection dropped briefly. Pull down on the room page to refresh, then try again. Volume and EQ changes go straight to the speaker over Wi-Fi — a weak signal can lose a command before the speaker receives it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My preset stopped being highlighted">
    The highlight means the room is still on the preset you loaded. It survives leaving the room, switching rooms and reopening the app; it clears when you move a slider the preset captured — in Somos InControl or the official Sonos® app — with a toast saying the preset was disabled, or when another preset loads. Tap the preset to reapply it. If a preset seems to load nothing, check what it captured: hold it and look at the ticks — Volume, Night Sound, Speech Boost and Loudness are only restored if they were ticked when it was saved.

    Can't see the TV presets at all? On a soundbar with **Music** lit, the room page shows only the header and volume — the music presets are on the music page. Tap **TV** to get the TV presets back. A room without a soundbar — a single speaker, a stereo pair, an Amp, even with a Sub — has no TV presets at all: its three presets are on the music page. See [TV rooms and music rooms](/rooms/overview#tv-rooms-and-music-rooms).

    A preset you saved for the first time loaded back as flat zeros? That was a bug in builds before 36.0.7 — the first save of a fresh slot kept the name but not the sound. It is fixed: hold the slot, name it, tap the button, and the sound you set is saved with it. Presets saved on an earlier build can be re-saved once to pick up the sound.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Sub phase is greyed out">
    The **Sub phase** switch (Normal / Reversed, under the Subwoofer switch on any room with a Sub) is set by Trueplay while Trueplay is on for that room — the note under it says so. Turn Trueplay off and the switch comes back. If it is greyed out with a spinner instead, the app is still waiting for the speaker to report its phase; pull down to refresh. See [Sub phase](/features/eq-and-trueplay#sub-phase).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A music service won't play, or the wrong room plays">
    - **"… isn't set up on your Sonos system yet"** — the Sonos® system has no account for that service. Add it in the Sonos® app once, then try again in Somos.
    - **Apple Music shows nothing to browse** — Somos needs Apple Music access on this iPhone. Tap **Allow** when asked, or turn it on under **Settings → Somos InControl**. A song that is only in your library and not in Apple's catalogue can't be sent to the speaker, and the page says so.
    - **The wrong room plays** — Somos plays to the room whose music page is open. The room name is under **Music** at the top of the page; swipe to another room or use the menu, then open Music there.
    - **The services list won't load** — Somos asks the room that leads the group; try again from a room that isn't part of a home theater, or tap **Add** again to re-read.
    - **Music started elsewhere but the app shows Standby** — the room page follows the speaker's real source on every change; pull down to refresh if it lags. See [Music Services](/features/music-services).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Speech Boost won't change">
    Somos InControl sets Speech Boost, reads the value back, and if the speaker hasn't accepted it the switch goes back and a toast says the speaker refused it — rather than pretending it worked. On the Arc Ultra, Speech Boost is a five-level slider (Off to Max) instead of a switch; if the slider doesn't hold a value, the speaker is reporting a different level than the one you set.

    Behaviour varies across soundbar models and the developer doesn't own every one. The fastest way to get it fixed is the questionnaire — every question is optional, including your email.

    <Card title="Speech Boost questionnaire" icon="message-question" href="https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/speech-boost.html">
      Answer a few quick questions about how Speech Boost behaves on your soundbar so the developer can reproduce and fix the issue.
    </Card>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Crossover won't change, or isn't there">
    From build 35.7.4, the crossover slider appears in **any room with a bonded Sub whose speaker answers for it** — not just a Sonos Amp. An earlier build limited it to the Amp, which took the control away from people who had been using it.

    If the slider is there but a change doesn't stick, your speaker is refusing the value. Sonos only documents an adjustable crossover on the Amp, so on other hardware the control is offered where the speaker reports one, and some models accept the reading but not the change. The app re-reads the value and shows what the speaker actually has rather than what you set.

    If the slider isn't there at all, the room's speaker didn't report a crossover when the app asked. Fill in the questionnaire either way — it's the only way this gets tracked down across hardware the developer doesn't own.

    Loading a preset said **"loaded, but crossover didn't take"**? Same cause — the speaker refused the crossover the preset carries. From 36.0.7 you see that message once; after that, presets in the room stop sending the crossover and load quietly. Moving the slider yourself, if the speaker accepts it, lets presets send it again. The slider itself is unchanged.

    <Card title="Crossover questionnaire" icon="message-question" href="https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/crossover.html">
      Answer a few quick questions about your Sub and the room it's bonded to so the developer can track down why the crossover isn't changing or isn't there.
    </Card>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Home theater configuration failed">
    Every speaker you assign must be completely standalone — not in any group, stereo pair, or bonded to another soundbar. Even partial group membership makes bonding fail. Ungroup everything in the official Sonos® app first, then try again. When a configuration doesn't take, Somos InControl restores your previous setup automatically — your system is never left half-configured.

    If a released speaker doesn't reappear as its own room after a minute, unplug it for 10 seconds and plug it back in — Sonos® firmware sometimes holds the old bond until the speaker restarts. See [Speaker Setup](/rooms/speaker-setup).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I started music and lost my TV sound">
    Playing music points the soundbar away from its TV input. Tap **TV** in the source row at the top of the room page — or the **TV** pill below the playback controls on the music page — to send it straight back. No need to touch the TV remote.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Live Activity isn't appearing">
    Check three things: the **Live Activity** switch under **System settings** in the app's menu is on; **Settings → Apps → Somos InControl → Live Activities** is enabled in iOS (the menu shows an orange link if it isn't); and your iPhone runs iOS 16.2 or later. The card also ends about 30 minutes after the app goes to the background — reopen the app to bring it back.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The widget shows nothing or old presets">
    Open Somos InControl at least once after installing the widget — the widget takes its rooms and presets from the app and refreshes each time you open it. To change which room or which row type it shows, long-press the widget and tap **Edit Widget**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The intro video keeps playing, or I want to see it again">
    The short intro video plays once, the first time the app opens after a fresh install, and never again on that install. If it keeps reappearing, there is likely an issue with local storage on your device — uninstall Somos InControl and reinstall it. To watch it again, the same thing: delete the app and reinstall. If you're signed in to iCloud your settings come back, but the video plays once more. From build 37 the video fills every iPhone and iPad screen, upright or sideways, with nothing cut off. On a Mac it is skipped, and the app opens straight on the setup pages. The old nine-step text tour that followed it is gone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pinch or the art swipe does nothing">
    Both gestures are off by default. Menu → **Rooms → System settings** → switch on **Pinch for volume** and **Swipe art to change songs**. The art swipe has to be a clear sideways swipe on the album art on the player: right for the next song, left for the previous. See [Gestures](/features/gestures).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Apple Watch app has no rooms, or can't reach them">
    Open Somos on your iPhone once — it sends your rooms to the Watch. If the Watch says it can't reach your Sonos, join your home Wi-Fi on the Watch or keep your iPhone nearby. Favourites arrive once the iPhone's music page has loaded them. See [Apple Watch](/features/apple-watch).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Somos isn't listed for my watch face, or the complication shows Open Somos">
    Open Somos on your iPhone, then open Somos on the Watch once — that gives the face a room to show. Then edit the face again: every slot lists all five Somos complications, under **Somos**. Tapping **Play / Pause** or **Next Room** opens the Watch app first; that is how Apple lets complications work. See [Watch Face](/features/watch-face).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My settings didn't come back, or don't match on my other device">
    - **Check iCloud.** Both devices must be signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID. Signed out, Somos keeps settings on the device only.
    - **Give it a minute.** After a reinstall, open Somos and wait. iCloud sends settings down in the background, and open screens update as they arrive.
    - **The first sync picks one device.** When two devices meet for the first time, one device's settings win for each item. After that, the newest edit wins.
    - **Some things never sync.** Room names live on the speakers. Music-service sign-ins, the Live Activity switch and the widget are set up on each device. See [iCloud Sync](/features/icloud-sync).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Whole Home didn't turn on after I bought it">
    Open the menu, tap **Whole Home**, then **Restore Purchases**. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. If a family organiser has to approve the purchase, the page says it's waiting for approval, and Whole Home turns on by itself once it goes through. See [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app crashes on launch">
    1. **Force-quit the app.** Swipe it away in the App Switcher, then tap the icon to reopen.
    2. **Restart your device.** If force-quitting doesn't help, a full device restart often will.
    3. **On a Mac, update.** Build 36.1.1 would not open on some Macs. From build 37 the Mac skips the intro video and opens straight on the setup pages, so install the latest build from TestFlight.
    4. **Contact support.** If the app still crashes after a restart, [send a message](/support/contact) with your device model and iOS version, and attach the log from **Submit diagnostics** in the menu if you can get that far.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
