**Try the assistant first.** The Ask button on this site answers most questions instantly, straight from the manual. If it can't help, send a message — Somos InControl is built and maintained by a single independent developer, and every message you send goes directly to that person. Not a support queue, not a bot: a real person reads it and a real person answers. Whether you've hit a bug, have a question the [FAQ](/support/faq) doesn't answer, or want to suggest what gets built next, this page is the right starting point. Replies typically arrive within 48 hours.

---

## Get help

Open the menu (top-left in the app) and tap **Support & Feedback** under Help. This opens the support form on the Somos website in your browser — the app itself sends nothing. You can also go straight to [somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/support.html](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/support.html).

Describe what you're seeing — the more detail you include, the faster the developer can reproduce and fix it. Your device model, iOS version, speaker models and the app build (shown at the bottom of the menu) are especially useful.

<Note>
  Name, email, subject and message are all required on the contact form — the email is what lets a real person reply to you.
</Note>

## Music services poll

A single top-level row in the menu — **Poll: your favorite music services** — opens a short poll on the Somos website in your browser — up to three services you type in. The app only opens the link; it sends nothing itself.
