Somos InControl is built and maintained by a single independent developer. Every message you send goes directly to that person — not a support queue, not a bot. Whether you've hit a bug, have a question the [FAQ](/support/faq) doesn't answer, or want to suggest what gets built next, this page is the right starting point. Replies typically arrive within 48 hours.

---

## Get help

Open the menu (top-left in the app) and tap **Support & Feedback** under Help. This opens the support form on the Somos website in your browser — the app itself sends nothing. You can also go straight to [somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/support.html](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/support.html).

Describe what you're seeing — the more detail you include, the faster the developer can reproduce and fix it. Your device model, iOS version, speaker models and the app build (shown at the bottom of the menu) are especially useful.

<Note>
  All fields in the contact form are optional. Leave the email field blank if you don't need a reply — your feedback still reaches the developer and helps improve the app.
</Note>

## Diagnostic questionnaires

A few specific issues are hard to reproduce without owning every Sonos® model, so the app asks a short set of questions when it sees the relevant hardware — an Arc Ultra, an Amp, two systems — rather than only when something fails. You'll be asked at most twice, never again once you've answered, and never more than once a week. The same four links live under **Speaker Checks** at the top of the menu. Every question in each form is optional, including your email address, and the app only opens a link — it sends nothing itself.
