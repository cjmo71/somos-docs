Somos InControl is built and maintained by a single independent developer. Every message you send goes directly to that person — not a support queue, not a bot. Whether you've hit a bug, have a question the [FAQ](/support/faq) doesn't answer, or want to suggest what gets built next, this page is the right starting point. Replies typically arrive within 48 hours.

---

## Get Help

Open the menu (top-left in the app) and tap **Support & Feedback** to reach the support form from inside the app. You can also access it directly on the web at [somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/support.html](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/support.html).

Describe what you're seeing — the more detail you include, the faster the developer can reproduce and fix it. Your device model and iOS version are especially useful for crash or compatibility reports.

<Note>
  All fields in the contact form are optional. Leave the email field blank if you don't need a reply — your feedback still reaches the developer and helps improve the app.
</Note>

## Suggest a Feature

Somos InControl's roadmap is shaped directly by what users ask for. There's no formal product plan — the app follows the direction its users point it in. Use the suggestions form (also accessible from the app menu) to add your idea to the list. Every submission is read and logged.

To see what's already been asked for and what's in progress, take a look at the roadmap below.

## Diagnostic Questionnaires

A few specific issues are hard to reproduce without owning every Sonos® model. If you've run into one of these, a short questionnaire is the fastest way to give the developer what's needed to fix it. Every question in each form is optional — including your email address.

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Speech Boost won't change" icon="waveform-lines" href="https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/speech-boost.html">
    Somos tried to toggle Speech Boost on your soundbar, read the value back, and found it hadn't taken. Answer a few questions about how Speech Boost behaves on your specific model to help the developer reproduce and fix it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Crossover won't change" icon="sliders" href="https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/crossover.html">
    The crossover control is available on the Sonos Amp. If the setting appears in the app but changes don't stick, this questionnaire captures the details needed to track down the cause.
  </Card>

  <Card title="A speaker or system didn't show up" icon="wifi-slash" href="https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/speakers-not-found.html">
    If Somos InControl found some of your speakers but not all — or missed an entire system — this questionnaire helps narrow down whether it's a network issue, an S1/S2 split, or something the app needs to handle differently.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
