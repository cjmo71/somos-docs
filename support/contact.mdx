**Try the assistant first.** The Ask button on this site answers most questions instantly, straight from the manual. If it can't help, send a message — Somos InControl is built and maintained by a single independent developer, and every message you send goes directly to that person. Not a support queue, not a bot: a real person reads it and a real person answers. Whether you've hit a bug, have a question the [FAQ](/support/faq) doesn't answer, or want to suggest what gets built next, this page is the right starting point. Replies typically arrive within 48 hours.

---

## Get help

Open the menu (top-left in the app) and tap **Support & Feedback** under **Help & Support**. This opens the support form on the Somos website in your browser — the app itself sends nothing. You can also go straight to [somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/support.html](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/support.html).

Describe what you're seeing — the more detail you include, the faster the developer can reproduce and fix it. Your device model, iOS version, speaker models and the app build (shown at the top of the menu) are especially useful.

### Send the app's log

When something went wrong, the log is worth more than a description. In the menu, under **Help us track bugs**, tap **Submit diagnostics**: the iOS share sheet opens straight away with the log attached as text, and you pick how to send it — Mail, Messages, whatever you use. The log holds the last few hundred lines of what the app did and what your speakers answered — action names, error codes, item ids — and never a password, a sign-in token or what you played. Send it soon after the problem; the log only keeps the recent past.

<Note>
  All fields in the contact form are optional. Leave the email field blank if you don't need a reply — your feedback still reaches the developer and helps improve the app.
</Note>

## Questionnaires and the poll on the website

A few specific issues are hard to reproduce without owning every Sonos® model, so the website has a short questionnaire for each. They are no longer in the app's menu or offered as popups — the app's own way to help is **Submit diagnostics** above — but the forms are still the best way to describe hardware the developer doesn't own. Every question in each form is optional, including your email address.

The app's menu does link to one thing on the site: **Poll: your favorite music services**, at the very top. Your answers decide which services get added next.
