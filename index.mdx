## What makes Somos InControl different

The official Sonos® app keeps adding screens, promotions, and loading states. Somos InControl does the opposite: one room at a time, the controls you actually reach for, and nothing else in the way.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="No account needed" icon="user-slash" href="/legal/privacy-policy">
    No sign-up, no Somos login, no servers of ours. Your speakers talk directly to the app over your local Wi-Fi; music services are signed in to on their own pages, never through us. Your settings sync through your own iCloud, if you're signed in.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Instant response" icon="bolt" href="/getting-started/first-launch">
    Every control applies immediately — no "Apply" button, no loading spinner. Changes made elsewhere show up within seconds.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Private by design" icon="shield-halved" href="/legal/data-and-network">
    Zero analytics, zero ads, zero third-party code. Your network stays your business.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the beta">
    Join the TestFlight beta at [testflight.apple.com/join/2D778AXH](https://testflight.apple.com/join/2D778AXH) and install Somos InControl on your iPhone or iPad.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk through setup and allow Local Network">
    On first launch, tap **Get started**, check the short list of what Sonos® needs, and tap **Allow** when iOS asks for **Local Network** permission — it's the only way the app can discover your Sonos® speakers. Then tap **Find My Speakers**. See [Permissions](/getting-started/permissions) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a room and start controlling">
    The app scans your network and opens your first room. Adjust volume, tune EQ, load a preset or open **Music** and play from one of your services — it all applies instantly. The short intro video you saw at first launch is the only tour; this manual covers the rest.
  </Step>
</Steps>
