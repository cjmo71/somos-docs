## What Makes Somos InControl Different

The official Sonos® app keeps adding screens, promotions, and loading states. Somos InControl does the opposite: one room at a time, the controls you actually reach for, and nothing else in the way.

## Get Started in Three Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the Beta">
    Join the TestFlight beta at [testflight.apple.com/join/2D778AXH](https://testflight.apple.com/join/2D778AXH) and install Somos InControl on your iPhone or iPad.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant Local Network Permission">
    On first launch, iOS asks for **Local Network** permission. Tap **Allow** — this is the only way the app can discover your Sonos® speakers. See [Permissions](/getting-started/permissions) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Room and Start Controlling">
    The app scans your network and lists every Sonos® room it finds. Tap a room and you're in. Adjust volume, tune EQ, or load a preset — it all applies instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
