## The Somos InControl Roadmap built by our users.

Somos is built in the open, shaped directly by what its users ask for. There is no formal roadmap — instead, we follow the direction of our users. Your suggestions are important, so please add yours to our list in the form below.

### Shipped — in the app now

- ✅ Support for all rooms, not just home theater — build 25 · July 8
- ✅ Stereo-pair balance slider — build 26 · July 10
- ✅ Single & mismatched surround speakers allowed — build 28 · July 12
- ✅ Surround & subwoofer level control — early July
- ✅ Music controls on the Lock Screen — build 29.3 · July 16
- ✅ Surround on/off toggle — build 31 · July 20
- ✅ Create stereo pairs in Somos — build 31 · July 20
- ✅ Faster, more reliable surround & fronts setup — build 31 · July 20
- ✅ Sliders apply live while you drag (the “volume delay” reports) — build 32 · July 21
- ✅ Max volume limit per room — build 32 · July 21
- ✅ Music Playback (Ambient/Full) surround toggle — build 32 · July 21
- ✅ S1 + S2 split-system support — rebuilt on Sonos household identity · build 32

### In the current beta — build 34, being tested now

Finished and in testers’ hands, not yet in the App Store version. [Ask for a beta invite](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/support.html#contact) if you want it early.

- 🧪 Split S1 + S2 systems: your S2 rooms no longer go missing
- 🧪 Speech Boost / dialogue enhancement now reports and sets correctly on Arc-class bars
- 🧪 Night Sound and Speech Boost work from the Lock Screen on older soundbars too
- 🧪 A second Sub, including two early-generation Subs
- 🧪 Presets can save the volume, not just the sound settings
- 🧪 Presets for music-only rooms, not just rooms with a soundbar
- 🧪 Mute straight from the volume card
- 🧪 Favourite groups on the bottom bar
- 🧪 Music and TV are a real toggle instead of looking disabled
- 🧪 Shuffle beside Play on the music page, and Play no longer turns your Repeat off
- 🧪 Portable speakers (Move, Move 2, Roam) show their battery level
- 🧪 Renaming a room can no longer wipe its icon if the speaker is slow to answer
- 🧪 Front-speaker information corrected — the Arc Ultra keeps its height channels
- 🧪 Crossover no longer resets to 0 Hz after a relaunch

### Still to come

- ◻️ Crossover control still goes missing for some systems — not solved; if this is you, the support form helps us more than anything
- ◻️ Line-in source selection — build 31 shows line-in on the right speaker; choosing it is still to do
- ◻️ Find-speaker tone (sound + light flash)
- ◻️ Distance settings for surrounds
- ◻️ System information view
- ◻️ Carousel room picker instead of tabs
- ◻️ Make favourite groups a list, in the app and on the web
- ◻️ Sleep timer — stop the music after a set time — researching
- ◻️ Separate TV and music speaker profiles — a manual switch is possible; detecting it automatically is not

### Asked for, but we can’t

Requested more than once. Sonos does not expose these, so no app can do them.

- ❌ A full graphic EQ — bass and treble are the only tone controls Sonos exposes
- ❌ Side-channel speakers, alongside fronts and rears

Found a bug instead? Use the [support form](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/support.html#contact) so it gets triaged as one.
