# Polls

One tap each. These decide what stays in the app and what goes.

#### Max volume limit

The per-room ceiling — hold the volume bar to set a level the room will not go past. Whether it stays depends on whether people use it.

[Vote →](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/max-volume.html)

Counts are tallied from the answers and refreshed through the day, so a vote shows up shortly rather than instantly.
