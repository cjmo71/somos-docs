Somos is free for one room, with everything that room can do. Whole Home gives every other room the full Somos, and lets them play together.

The free room gets presets, EQ, the Sub, surrounds and front speakers, music, the Lock Screen card, the Home Screen widgets, Siri, the Apple Watch and iCloud sync, for as long as you like. **Whole Home** is a one-time purchase that opens up the rest of your home.

<Note>
  **On TestFlight, Whole Home works just as it will on the App Store:** one room is free and the rest are locked until you buy. In TestFlight it's free: tap **Buy** and you won't be charged. A purchase in TestFlight is a free test purchase and doesn't carry over to the App Store version.
</Note>

## What Whole Home adds

- **Every room, fully loaded:** presets, EQ, sub and surround tuning, music and Apple Watch in every room, not just one
- **Group rooms:** play the same thing anywhere, with one volume for the group and each room's own level
- **Saved groups:** save "Downstairs" or "Whole House" and bring it back with one tap on the wheel
- **Yours for good:** one purchase, no subscription, shared with your family through Family Sharing

|  | Free | Whole Home |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One room, with everything that room can do | Yes | Yes |
| Home Screen widgets, Siri and the Apple Watch | Yes | Yes |
| Every other room in your home | — | Yes |
| Grouping rooms and saved groups | — | Yes |

- **A one-room home is never asked.** With only one room, there is nothing more to open, and Somos never shows the offer.
- **Your free room** is the one you choose. With two or more rooms, Somos asks you to **Pick your free room** when it first finds your speakers. You can change it once, with **Change free room** on the Whole Home page. If your free room leaves your home, Somos asks you to pick again.

## Buying and restoring

Whole Home has its own page in the menu. Open the menu (top-left) and tap **Unlock Whole Home**, the row under the **Upgrade** heading, with the price beside it. Once you own it, the row reads **Manage Whole Home**. **Restore Purchases** is on that page too.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buy">
    Tap **Buy for** and the price, then confirm with the App Store. The page changes to **Thank you for buying Whole Home**, with the date you bought it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore Purchases">
    On a new device, or after a reinstall, tap **Restore Purchases**. The App Store checks your Apple Account and turns Whole Home back on. If there is nothing to restore, the page says **No Whole Home purchase was found for this Apple Account.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Apple asks for your Apple Account password to confirm a purchase. That box is Apple's, not ours. To use Face ID instead, turn on **Settings › Face ID & Passcode › iTunes & App Store** ([Apple's page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108411)).
</Tip>

If a family organiser has to approve the purchase (Ask to Buy), the page says **Your purchase is waiting for approval.** Whole Home turns on by itself once it goes through.

## When you open a locked room

Without Whole Home, every room except your free one is locked, and a lock shows beside it in the menu's room list. Open a locked room and its page shows the room's name and a lock. It says **Your free room is** and the free room's name, with a **Go to** button that takes you there. Under that, it points you to **Whole Home** in the menu. Nothing about your free room changes.

## When you reach something else Whole Home covers

Without Whole Home, tapping the group button or a saved group opens a short Whole Home page instead. It says what you tapped, lists what Whole Home adds, and has the same **Buy** and **Restore Purchases** buttons. Tap **Not now** to go back to your free room.

<Tip>
  The purchase belongs to your Apple Account, not to the [iCloud copy of your settings](/features/icloud-sync). It works on every iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to that account. Use **Restore Purchases** if a device doesn't pick it up on its own.
</Tip>
