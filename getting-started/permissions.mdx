Somos InControl asks for two iOS permissions on first launch: Local Network and Bluetooth. Neither permission is used for tracking, analytics, or anything that leaves your home network. They exist for a single practical reason — finding and talking to your Sonos® speakers. Here's exactly what each permission does, what happens if you decline it, and how to turn it back on if you change your mind.

## Local Network Permission

### What it does

iOS requires any app that discovers or communicates with devices on your Wi-Fi to request Local Network permission. Somos InControl uses this permission for two things:

- **Bonjour (mDNS) discovery** — the app listens for Sonos® speakers advertising the `_sonos._tcp` service on your network, the same discovery method used by AirPlay and the official Sonos® app.
- **Direct subnet probe** — as a fallback, the app sends a short request to each address on your local subnet asking "are you a Sonos® speaker?" Any Sonos® device that responds is added to your room list.

Once speakers are discovered, all control traffic — volume changes, EQ adjustments, presets, home theater commands — also travels over your local Wi-Fi using this permission.

### What it does NOT do

Local Network permission only covers devices on the Wi-Fi network your phone is currently connected to. It does not give the app access to the internet, to your router's admin interface, or to any device on a different network.

<Note>
  Local Network permission is **required**. If you decline it, the app cannot find any Sonos® speakers and will have nothing to control. Bluetooth is optional — the app works fully without it for speakers already on your Wi-Fi.
</Note>

### What happens if you decline

If you tap **Don't Allow** when iOS asks for Local Network permission, the app opens but finds no rooms. No speakers will appear, and no controls will be available.

### How to re-enable Local Network permission

If you declined Local Network permission and want to grant it later:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open iOS Settings">
    Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone or iPad.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Somos InControl">
    Scroll down the Settings list until you see **Somos InControl** and tap it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Local Network">
    Tap the toggle next to **Local Network** to turn it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to the app">
    Switch back to Somos InControl. Force-quit and reopen the app if your rooms don't appear immediately — the app runs discovery fresh on each launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bluetooth Permission

### What it does

The app uses Bluetooth for one specific purpose: detecting Sonos® speakers that are **nearby but not yet connected to your Wi-Fi network** — for example, a brand-new speaker you're in the process of setting up, or a speaker that has temporarily gone offline.

When the permission is granted, the app only reads the advertising name broadcast by nearby Bluetooth devices and filters for names that match Sonos® hardware. It does not:

- Pair with any device
- Send any data over Bluetooth
- Identify you, your device, or your location
- Track movement or proximity over time

### What happens if you decline

If you decline Bluetooth permission, the app continues to work normally for every speaker that is already on your Wi-Fi. You simply won't see the detection prompt for nearby speakers that aren't yet on the network.

<Note>
  Bluetooth permission is **optional**. All core features — volume, EQ, presets, home theater controls, Live Activity — work without it.
</Note>

### How to re-enable Bluetooth permission

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open iOS Settings">
    Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone or iPad.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Somos InControl">
    Scroll down to **Somos InControl** and tap it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Bluetooth">
    Tap the toggle next to **Bluetooth** to turn it on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Permission Summary

| Permission | Required? | Used for | Works without it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local Network | **Yes** | Discovering and controlling Sonos® speakers on your Wi-Fi | ❌ No speakers will be found |
| Bluetooth | No | Detecting nearby Sonos® speakers not yet on Wi-Fi | ✅ Yes, for speakers already on the network |

<Info>
  Somos InControl contains zero third-party libraries and makes no analytics or telemetry requests. The only outbound internet traffic is a single image fetch from Sonos®'s public photo library (`media.sonos.com`) to display speaker photos. See the full [Privacy Policy](https://somosincontrol.com/somos-incontrol/privacy.html) for details.
</Info>
