Everything that isn't a room control lives in the menu. Tap the **menu button** in the top-left corner of any room page to open it; tap outside, pick a room, or tap the down arrow at the top right to close it. The top of the menu is slim on purpose: the app icon and the name, so the rows below get the room. The version number is at the bottom, under **About**.

Anything with more than a line or two to say opens as a pop-up, so the menu itself stays short.

## Sections, top to bottom

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Rooms (hold to reorder)">
    First on the menu. With four rooms or fewer they are listed right here: **tap** a room to switch to it, **hold** and drag to change the order. With more than four, one **Rooms** row shows how many you have and opens them in a pop-up. There, your four favourites (the bottom tabs) sit in their own box above the rest. Hold a room and drag to reorder, or drag it between the boxes to change your four favourites. See [Favorites](/rooms/favorites).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sleep timer">
    Its own row, for the room you have open. It opens a pop-up with **Off**, **15 min**, **30 min**, **45 min**, **60 min** and **90 min**. While a timer runs, the row shows the minutes left. See [Sleep Timer](/features/sleep-timer).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Whole Home">
    One row in its own box, under an **Upgrade** heading, with the App Store's price beside it. It opens the Whole Home page: what the one-time purchase adds, with **Buy** and **Restore Purchases**. Once you own it, the heading goes and the row just reads **Whole Home**. See [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="System">
    Your Sonos® system's name and generation (S1 / S2), or a switcher when there's more than one — press and hold a system to rename it. **Add a Home / New S1 or S2 System** finds another system; **Rescan My Network** looks again for anything that didn't show up. See [Multiple Systems](/rooms/multiple-systems).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="System settings">
    Its own box, directly under System. The row opens a pop-up with:

    - **Live Activity** — Lock Screen and Dynamic Island controls, off by default. See [Live Activity](/features/live-activity). Not shown on a Mac, where Live Activities can't run.
    - **Haptics** — the little taps you feel on slider steps and buttons.
    - **Appearance** — System, Light or Dark.
    - **Pinch for volume** — off by default. See [Gestures](/features/gestures).
    - **Swipe art to change songs** — off by default. See [Gestures](/features/gestures).
    - **Room wheel** — off by default. Spin a wheel on the bottom bar to pick a room, instead of tapping tabs. See [Favorites](/rooms/favorites#the-room-wheel).
    - **Open iOS Settings for Somos** — jumps to the app's own page in iOS Settings, where Local Network, Bluetooth and Apple Music access live.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Help & Support">
    Flat rows, no folder:

    - **Settings Somos needs** — the checklist of what must be true outside Somos: UPnP on in the Sonos® app, VPN off, the same Wi-Fi as the speakers, not a guest or IoT network. See [First Launch](/getting-started/first-launch).
    - **Getting Started** and **Feature Reference** — open this manual in your browser.
    - **Apple Watch** — a pop-up with **Add Infograph face** (and **Add Modular Ultra face** on an Apple Watch Ultra), how to install the Watch app and add complications, and **Apple Watch guide**, which opens the manual's [Apple Watch](/features/apple-watch) page. See [Watch Face](/features/watch-face).
    - **Support & Feedback** and **Suggest a Feature** — open the support and suggestions forms on the Somos website.
    - **Poll: your favorite music services** — a one-page poll on the Somos website. Your answers decide which music services get added next. The app only opens the link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Help us track bugs">
    **Submit diagnostics** — opens the iOS share sheet straight away with the app's log attached, so you can send it by mail or message when something went wrong. The log holds the last few hundred lines of what the app did and what the speakers answered — never a password, a sign-in token or your music. See [Contact](/support/contact).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="About">
    - **Privacy, Trademark & About** — the privacy summary, trademark notice and the story behind the name.
    - **Version** — the app's version and build number, for example **Version 1.1 (37.8.2)**. Handy when you contact support.
    - **iCloud** — whether your settings are syncing: **iCloud: synced 2 min ago** after a sync, **iCloud: on** while signed in and waiting for the first one, or **iCloud: off** when the device isn't signed in to iCloud. See [iCloud Sync](/features/icloud-sync).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Somos InControl has no in-app account and no settings beyond what's listed here. Its one purchase is [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home). Presets, room order, favorites and the switches above are kept on the device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, synced to your other devices — see [iCloud Sync](/features/icloud-sync). Music-service sign-ins stay on the device — see [Data & Network](/legal/data-and-network).
</Tip>
