Everything that isn't a room control lives in the menu. Tap the **menu button** in the top-left corner of any room page to open it; tap outside, pick a room, or tap the down arrow at the top right to close it. The top of the menu is slim on purpose: the app icon and the name, so the rows below get the room. The version number is at the bottom, under **About**.

Every section has a heading, and each row says what it does. Anything with more than a line or two to say opens as a pop-up, so the menu itself stays short. When you tap **Done** on a pop-up, you land back on the open menu.

## Sections, top to bottom

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Rooms">
    One row under the **Rooms** heading: **See all** and the number of rooms (**See all 6 rooms**, for example). It opens a **Rooms** pop-up that lists every room under **All rooms**, with a tick beside the room you have open. **Tap** a room to switch to it. The menu does not reorder rooms: the order of the room tabs is the only order, and you change it there. See [Room Tabs & Saved Groups](/rooms/favorites).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sleep timer">
    Under the **Sleep timer** heading, for the room you have open. The row reads **Set a sleep timer**; while a timer runs, it also shows **Stops in** and the minutes left. It opens a pop-up with **Off**, **15 min**, **30 min**, **45 min**, **60 min** and **90 min**. See [Sleep Timer](/features/sleep-timer).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Whole Home">
    One row in its own box, under an **Upgrade** heading. It reads **Unlock Whole Home** with the App Store's price beside it. It opens the Whole Home page: what the one-time purchase adds, with **Buy** and **Restore Purchases**. Once you own it, the heading changes to **Whole Home** and the row reads **Manage Whole Home**. See [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your Sonos">
    Your Sonos® system's name and generation (S1 / S2), or a switcher when there's more than one — press and hold a system to rename it. **Add a Home** finds another system; **Rescan My Network** looks again for anything that didn't show up. In Demo Mode there is no Sonos® system, so this section is empty and its heading is hidden. See [Multiple Systems](/rooms/multiple-systems).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="App">
    Three rows about the app itself, not your speakers:

    - **Somos settings** — a pop-up with the app's own switches:
      - **Somos Live Activity** — Lock Screen and Dynamic Island controls, off by default. See [Live Activity](/features/live-activity). Not shown on a Mac, where Live Activities can't run. If iOS has Live Activities turned off for Somos, the switch is replaced by **Off in iOS Settings** and an **Open iOS Settings** button.
      - **Haptics** — the little taps you feel on slider steps and buttons.
      - **Appearance** — System, Light or Dark. The change applies to everything at once, including the open pop-up.
      - **Pinch for volume** — off by default. See [Gestures](/features/gestures).
      - **Swipe art to change songs** — off by default. See [Gestures](/features/gestures).
      - **Room wheel** — off by default. Spin a wheel on the bottom bar to pick a room, instead of tapping tabs. See [Room Tabs & Saved Groups](/rooms/favorites#the-room-wheel).
      - **Open iOS Settings for Somos** — jumps to the app's own page in iOS Settings, where Local Network, Bluetooth and Apple Music access live.
    - **Apple Watch** — a pop-up with the steps to install Somos on your watch, then **Add Infograph face** (and **Add Modular Ultra face** on an Apple Watch Ultra), how to add complications, and **Apple Watch guide**, which opens the manual's [Apple Watch](/features/apple-watch) page. See [Watch Face](/features/watch-face).
    - **Set up Somos iCloud** — shows whether your settings are syncing on the right of the row: **iCloud: synced 2 min ago** after a sync, **iCloud: on** while signed in and waiting for the first one, or **iCloud: off** when the device isn't signed in to iCloud. Tap it for the short steps to turn Somos on in iOS Settings, and an **Open iOS Settings** button. See [iCloud Sync](/features/icloud-sync).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Help & Support">
    Flat rows, no folder:

    - **Settings Somos needs** — the checklist of what must be true outside Somos: UPnP on in the Sonos® app, a VPN that allows your home network, the same Wi-Fi as the speakers, not a guest or IoT network. See [First Launch](/getting-started/first-launch).
    - **Getting Started** and **Feature Reference** — open this manual in a pop-up inside the app. Tap **Done** to come back to Somos.
    - **Support & Feedback** and **Suggest a Feature** — open the support and suggestions forms on the Somos website in your browser.
    - **Poll: your favorite music services** — a one-page poll on the Somos website. Your answers decide which music services get added next. The app only opens the link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Help us track bugs">
    **Submit diagnostics** — opens an email to Somos Support, with the subject **Somos diagnostics** and the app's log attached, so you can send it when something went wrong. If Mail isn't set up on the device, the iOS share sheet opens with the log instead, and you pick how to send it. The log holds the last few hundred lines of what the app did and what the speakers answered — never a password, a sign-in token or your music. Nothing is sent until you send it. See [Contact](/support/contact).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="About">
    - **Privacy & Trademarks** — opens **Privacy, Trademark & About**: the privacy summary, trademark notice and the story behind the name.
    - **Version** — the app's version and build number, for example **Version 1.1 (37.8.2)**. Handy when you contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Somos InControl has no in-app account and no settings beyond what's listed here. Its one purchase is [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home). Sound presets, room order, favorites and the switches above are kept on the device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, synced to your other devices — see [iCloud Sync](/features/icloud-sync). Music-service sign-ins stay on the device — see [Data & Network](/legal/data-and-network).
</Tip>
