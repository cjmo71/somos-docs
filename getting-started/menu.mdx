Everything that isn't a room control lives in the menu. Tap the **menu button** in the top-left corner of any room page to open it; tap outside, pick a room, or tap the down arrow at the top right to close it. The top of the menu is slim on purpose: the app icon and the name, so the rows below get the room. The version number is on the **About** page.

Anything with more than a line or two to say opens as a pop-up or as a page of its own, so the menu itself stays short. **Your Sonos**, **App**, **Help & Support** and **About** each open a page; tap the back arrow to return to the menu.

## Sections, top to bottom

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Rooms">
    First on the menu. One row, **See all** and the number of rooms (for example **See all 5 rooms**), opens a pop-up with every room. **Tap** a room to switch to it. A room that Whole Home hasn't opened has a lock beside it. To change the order of your rooms, press and hold a tab on the bottom bar. See [Favorites](/rooms/favorites).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Whole Home">
    One row in its own box. Until you own it, the box has an **Upgrade** heading and the row reads **Unlock Whole Home** with the App Store's price beside it. It opens the Whole Home page: what the one-time purchase adds, with **Buy** and **Restore Purchases**. Once you own it, the heading goes and the row reads **Manage Whole Home**. See [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your Sonos">
    Opens a page with your Sonos® system's name and generation (S1 / S2), or a switcher when there's more than one — press and hold a system to rename it. **Add a Home / New S1 or S2 System** finds another system; **Rescan My Network** looks again for anything that didn't show up. See [Multiple Systems](/rooms/multiple-systems).

    **Set a sleep timer** is here too, for the room you have open. It opens a pop-up with **Off**, **15 min**, **30 min**, **45 min**, **60 min** and **90 min**. While a timer runs, the row shows the minutes left. See [Sleep Timer](/features/sleep-timer).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="App">
    Opens a page with two rows: **Somos settings** and **Apple Watch**.

    **Somos settings** opens a pop-up with:

    - **Live Activity** — Lock Screen and Dynamic Island controls, off by default. See [Live Activity](/features/live-activity). Not shown on a Mac, where Live Activities can't run.
    - **Haptics** — the little taps you feel on slider steps and buttons.
    - **Appearance** — System, Light or Dark.
    - **Pinch for volume** — off by default. See [Gestures](/features/gestures).
    - **Swipe art to change songs** — off by default. See [Gestures](/features/gestures).
    - **Room wheel** — off by default. Spin a wheel on the bottom bar to pick a room, instead of tapping tabs. See [Favorites](/rooms/favorites#the-room-wheel).
    - **Open iOS Settings for Somos** — jumps to the app's own page in iOS Settings, where Local Network, Bluetooth and Apple Music access live.

    **Apple Watch** opens a pop-up with **Add Infograph face** (and **Add Modular Ultra face** on an Apple Watch Ultra), how to install the Watch app and add complications, and **Apple Watch guide**, which opens the manual's [Apple Watch](/features/apple-watch) page. See [Watch Face](/features/watch-face).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Help & Support">
    One row that opens a page with:

    - **Settings Somos needs** — the checklist of what must be true outside Somos: UPnP on in the Sonos® app, a VPN that allows your home network, the same Wi-Fi as the speakers, not a guest or IoT network. See [If Somos can't find your speakers](/getting-started/first-launch#if-somos-cant-find-your-speakers).
    - **Getting Started** and **Feature Reference** — open this manual inside the app.
    - **Visit our website** — more support options and user input, on the Somos website in your browser.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Submit diagnostics">
    Its own row in its own box. Tap it and, when Mail is set up on your iPhone, an email opens with the app's log attached, ready to send to the developer; otherwise the iOS share sheet opens with the log so you can send it by message or any other way. Use it when something went wrong. The log holds the last few hundred lines of what the app did and what the speakers answered — never a password, a sign-in token or your music. See [Contact](/support/contact).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="About">
    The row carries the app's icon. It opens a page with:

    - **Privacy & Trademarks** — opens the privacy summary, trademark notice and the story behind the name.
    - **Version** — the app's version and build number, for example **Version 1.1 (39.0.4)**. Handy when you contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Somos InControl has no in-app account and no settings beyond what's listed here. Its one purchase is [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home). Presets, room order, favorites and the switches above are kept on the device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, synced to your other devices — see [iCloud Sync](/features/icloud-sync). Music-service sign-ins stay on the device — see [Data & Network](/legal/data-and-network).
</Tip>
