Everything that isn't a room control lives in the menu. Tap the **menu button** in the top-left corner of any room page to open it; tap outside, or pick a room, to close it. The app's version and build number are shown at the bottom.

## Sections, top to bottom

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="System">
    Your Sonos® system's name and generation (S1 / S2), or a switcher when there's more than one. **Add a Home / New S1 or S2 System** finds another system; **Advanced → Rescan My Network** looks again for anything that didn't show up. See [Multiple Systems](/rooms/multiple-systems).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Settings">
    - **Live Activity** — Lock Screen and Dynamic Island controls, off by default. See [Live Activity](/features/live-activity).
    - **Haptics** — the little taps you feel on slider steps and buttons.
    - **Appearance** — System, Light or Dark.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rooms">
    Every room on the active system. Tap to switch, touch and hold to drag into your preferred order — the top 4 become the bottom tabs — and swipe left on a room to rename it. See [Favorites](/rooms/favorites).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="App Permissions">
    What Local Network and Bluetooth are for, in plain language, plus an **Open iOS Settings for Somos** button that jumps straight to the app's page in iOS Settings. The footer repeats the four things Sonos needs from [First Launch](/getting-started/first-launch). See [Permissions](/getting-started/permissions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Poll: your favorite music services">
    A single row, not a submenu. Tap it to open the music-services poll on the Somos website in your browser — the app itself sends nothing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Help">
    - **Take the Tour** — replays the nine-step onboarding tour.
    - **Getting Started** — a short guide covering permissions and discovery; opens the manual on the website in your browser.
    - **Feature Reference** — the detailed setup guide with step-by-step sections for discovery, controls, presets, home theater, music, Live Activity, the widget and an FAQ; also opens on the website.
    - **What's Coming** — opens the "Coming soon" section of the Somos website.
    - A line pointing you to the manual's assistant first, and the support form if that doesn't answer it.
    - **Support & Feedback** and **Suggest a Feature** — open the support and suggestions forms on the Somos website.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Privacy, Trademark & About (Legal & Info)">
    Opens the **Legal & Info** sheet: the privacy summary, trademark notice and app information.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Somos InControl has no in-app account, no in-app purchases, and no settings beyond what's listed here. Presets, room order and favourites are stored on the device and shared with the widget — see [Data & Network](/legal/data-and-network).
</Tip>
