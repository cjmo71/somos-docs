Everything that isn't a room control lives in the menu. Tap the **menu button** in the top-left corner of any room page to open it; tap outside, or pick a room, to close it. The app's version and build number are shown at the top, under the name.

## Sections, top to bottom

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Rooms (hold to reorder)">
    First on the menu. Every room on the active system. **Tap** a room to switch to it; **hold** and drag to change the order — the top 4 become the bottom tabs, and with more than four rooms a divider separates them from the rest. Renaming is done on the room page (press and hold the room name), not here. See [Favorites](/rooms/favorites).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Poll and Coming Soon">
    - **Poll: your favorite music services** — a one-page poll on the Somos website. Your answers decide which music services get added next. The app only opens the link.
    - **Coming Soon** — what is being worked on, on the website.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Whole Home">
    One row in its own box, directly above System. It opens the Whole Home page: what the one-time purchase adds (every other room, grouping, the Home Screen widgets and front speakers), with **Buy**, **Redeem a code** and **Restore Purchases**. See [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="System">
    Your Sonos® system's name and generation (S1 / S2), or a switcher when there's more than one — press and hold a system to rename it. **Add a Home / New S1 or S2 System** finds another system; **Rescan My Network** looks again for anything that didn't show up. See [Multiple Systems](/rooms/multiple-systems).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="System settings">
    Its own box, directly under System. The row folds out to:

    - **Live Activity** — Lock Screen and Dynamic Island controls, off by default. See [Live Activity](/features/live-activity).
    - **Haptics** — the little taps you feel on slider steps and buttons.
    - **Appearance** — System, Light or Dark.
    - **Pinch for volume** — off by default. See [Gestures](/features/gestures).
    - **Swipe art to change songs** — off by default. See [Gestures](/features/gestures).
    - **Room wheel** — off by default. Spin a wheel on the bottom bar to pick a room, instead of tapping tabs. See [Favorites](/rooms/favorites#the-room-wheel).
    - **Open iOS Settings for Somos** — jumps to the app's own page in iOS Settings, where Local Network, Bluetooth and Apple Music access live.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Help & Support">
    Flat rows, no folder:

    - **Settings Somos needs** — the checklist of what must be true outside Somos: UPnP on in the Sonos® app, VPN off, the same Wi-Fi as the speakers, not a guest or IoT network. See [First Launch](/getting-started/first-launch).
    - **Getting Started** and **Feature Reference** — open this manual in your browser.
    - **Apple Watch** — opens the manual's [Apple Watch](/features/apple-watch) page: installing the Watch app and what it does on a TV room and a music room.
    - **Support & Feedback** and **Suggest a Feature** — open the support and suggestions forms on the Somos website.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Help us track bugs">
    **Submit diagnostics** — opens the iOS share sheet straight away with the app's log attached, so you can send it by mail or message when something went wrong. The log holds the last few hundred lines of what the app did and what the speakers answered — never a password, a sign-in token or your music. See [Contact](/support/contact).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Legal & Info">
    **Privacy, Trademark & About** — the privacy summary, trademark notice and the story behind the name.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Somos InControl has no in-app account and no settings beyond what's listed here. Its one purchase is [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home). Presets, room order, favorites and the switches above are kept on the device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, synced to your other devices — see [iCloud Sync](/features/icloud-sync). Music-service sign-ins stay on the device — see [Data & Network](/legal/data-and-network).
</Tip>
