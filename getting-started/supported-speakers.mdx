Somos InControl works with any Sonos® speaker that supports local control, on S1 or S2 systems — and with more than one system at a time. If a speaker shows up in the official Sonos app on your Wi-Fi, it shows up here too.

## What every room gets

Regardless of model, every room gets:

- Volume slider with − / +, mute, and group volume
- Bass and treble EQ, Loudness
- Sound presets you save and switch
- Trueplay, Status Light and Touch Controls tiles
- The Music sheet with favorites, playlists and the queue
- A room diagram, room grouping, and room rename

The table below is what each model adds **on top of that** — and which Sonos system it runs on.

## Speaker, system, and what's added

<Note>
  Speech Boost's five-step slider is the app asking each soundbar whether it supports it — confirmed on the Arc Ultra. On every other bar, Speech Boost is an on/off tile. Battery reporting on Move / Roam models uses the same status endpoint Sonos's own apps read, but it hasn't yet been confirmed against real portable hardware in-house.
</Note>

Balance, height and crossover only appear once the hardware is actually there — a stereo pair, an Atmos-capable bar, or a bonded Sub that answers back. See [Home Theater](/features/home-theater) and [EQ &amp; Trueplay](/features/eq-and-trueplay) for how those controls behave once they show up.
