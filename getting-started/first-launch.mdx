The first time you open Somos InControl you get a short set of full-screen pages — one thing per page — that end with one big button: **Find My Speakers**. The iOS permission prompts are triggered from those pages, never before. On a fresh install a short intro video plays once, before those pages. It fills the screen on every iPhone and iPad, upright or sideways, with nothing cut off. On a Mac there is no video: the app opens straight on the setup pages. The whole thing takes under a minute, and you don't need a Sonos® account or any prior setup.

<Note>
  Somos InControl requires **Local Network** permission to find your Sonos® speakers. Without it, the app has nothing to control. See [App Permissions](/getting-started/permissions) for what each permission does and how to re-enable it if you declined.
</Note>

## What setup asks of you

Somos talks to your speakers over your home Wi-Fi and nothing else. Setup is one page per thing: **Get started**, then **Allow Somos to find your speakers** (the Local Network prompt), then **Let Somos see nearby speakers** (the Bluetooth prompt), then **Find My Speakers**. There is no checklist to read first. If Somos can't find your speakers, it shows you what to check — see [If Somos can't find your speakers](#if-somos-cant-find-your-speakers) below.

<Note>
  Bluetooth is **optional** — Somos works over Wi-Fi alone. It only helps spot a speaker that is on but not on your Wi-Fi yet.
</Note>

## Download and set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install TestFlight">
    Somos InControl is in public beta and distributed through Apple's TestFlight platform. If you don't have it already, download **TestFlight** for free from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the beta">
    Open this link on your device: **[testflight.apple.com/join/2D778AXH](https://testflight.apple.com/join/2D778AXH)**

    Tap **Accept** on the TestFlight page, then tap **Install** to download Somos InControl. TestFlight will notify you when updates are available. The Apple Watch app installs alongside it — see [Apple Watch](/features/apple-watch).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get started">
    Open the app. The first page is the name and a line about what it does. Tap **Get started**. If you just want to look around without any Sonos® hardware, tap **Explore Demo Mode** instead — three demo rooms appear, and an **Exit Demo** button at the top of each page takes you back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow Somos to find your speakers">
    The next page is **Allow Somos to find your speakers**. Tap **Continue** and iOS asks whether to allow the app to find and connect to devices on your local network. Tap **Allow**.

    This lets the app discover and control your Sonos® speakers over Wi-Fi. It does not give the app access to any device outside your home network. If you tap **Don't Allow** by mistake, the app shows you how to re-enable it, with a **Try Again** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Somos see nearby speakers (optional)">
    Tap **Continue** and iOS asks for Bluetooth. This is used only to spot Sonos® speakers that are nearby but not yet on your Wi-Fi — a new speaker you're about to set up. Tap **Allow** for that, or **Don't Allow** to skip it. The app works fully without it for any speaker already on your network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find My Speakers">
    The last page says Somos will now look for your Sonos® speakers on this Wi-Fi. Tap **Find My Speakers**. The app searches your subnet and listens for Bonjour at the same time; on most home networks this takes a few seconds. Every Sonos® room appears — soundbar rooms, stereo pairs, single and portable speakers — and the first one opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="That's it">
    Your first room is open. There is no tour to click through — the short intro video you saw on first open (on iPhone and iPad) is the only walkthrough, and this manual is the reference from then on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If discovery found two systems, or suspects one is missing, the app asks **Did we find everything?** once your rooms are on screen. See [Multiple Systems](/rooms/multiple-systems).

<Note>
  On a Mac, a page called **Settings Somos needs** comes between **Get started** and the permission pages. iPhone and iPad go straight to **Allow Somos to find your speakers**.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Reinstalling, or setting up a second iPhone, iPad or Mac? If you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID, your presets, room order, saved groups, favorites and switches come back on their own within a minute of opening the app. See [iCloud Sync](/features/icloud-sync).
</Tip>

Somos is free for one room. Every other room, grouping and saved groups are part of [Whole Home](/getting-started/whole-home), a one-time purchase. With two or more rooms, Somos asks you to pick your free room. On TestFlight, **Buy** is a free test purchase.

## If Somos can't find your speakers

If the search finds nothing, you land on a **No speakers found** screen. Under **If Somos can't find your speakers, check:** it lists the four things that have to be true outside Somos:

1. **UPnP on in the Sonos® app.** Sonos app › Account › Privacy & Security › UPnP › on.
2. **VPN allows your home network.** If the Sonos app works with your VPN on, Somos will too.
3. **Phone on the same Wi-Fi as the speakers.** Not cellular.
4. **Not a guest or IoT network.** Those keep devices from seeing each other.

Fix whichever one applies, then tap **Try Again**. **Open Settings** takes you to Somos's page in iOS Settings, where you can turn **Local Network** back on if you declined it. If Local Network is off, the screen also says **Local Network access is required** and shows where to switch it on.

The same four checks are always available in the menu under **Help & Support → Settings Somos needs**.

## Help after setup

Open the menu (top-left) and tap **Help & Support**. It opens a page with **Settings Somos needs** (the four checks above), **Getting Started** and **Feature Reference** (both open this manual), and **Visit our website** for more support options. The ready-made Somos watch face is under **App → Apple Watch**. See [The Menu](/getting-started/menu).

## What gets discovered

Every Sonos® room on your Wi-Fi appears automatically — you don't choose rooms manually. What you can control depends on what's in each room:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="All rooms" icon="sliders" href="/rooms/overview">
    Volume, mute, bass, treble, loudness, presets, Trueplay, Status Light, Touch Controls, the music page with your music services, grouping and rename.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Soundbar rooms" icon="tv" href="/features/home-theater">
    Everything above, plus TV / Music source, the audio-format badge, Night Sound, Speech Boost, height, surround and home theater setup.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rooms with a subwoofer" icon="volume-high" href="/features/eq-and-trueplay">
    Any room with a bonded Sub — soundbar or not — adds the subwoofer level, on/off, and the crossover wherever the speaker reports one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your rooms on the bottom bar" icon="star" href="/rooms/favorites">
    The bottom bar holds your rooms as tabs (swipe them to change room), plus a second page of saved groups. Hold a tab to reorder or rename it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  If a room isn't appearing, make sure your iPhone or iPad is on the **same Wi-Fi network** as your Sonos® speakers — not a guest network or an IoT VLAN. Then open the menu and tap **Your Sonos → Rescan My Network**.
</Tip>
