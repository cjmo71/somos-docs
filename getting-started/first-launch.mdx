The first time you open Somos InControl, two things happen in quick succession: iOS asks for permission to access your local network, and then a short interactive tour walks you through every part of the app. From there the app scans your Wi-Fi and lists every Sonos® room it finds — the whole process takes under a minute, and you don't need a Sonos® account or any prior setup.

<Note>
  Somos InControl requires **Local Network** permission to find your Sonos® speakers. Without it, the app has nothing to control. See [App Permissions](/getting-started/permissions) for details on what the permission does and how to re-enable it if you declined.
</Note>

## Download and Set Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install TestFlight">
    Somos InControl is in public beta and distributed through Apple's TestFlight platform. If you don't have it already, download **TestFlight** for free from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the beta">
    Open this link on your device: **[testflight.apple.com/join/2D778AXH](https://testflight.apple.com/join/2D778AXH)**

    Tap **Accept** on the TestFlight page, then tap **Install** to download Somos InControl. TestFlight will notify you when updates are available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant Local Network permission">
    The first time you open Somos InControl, iOS will show a prompt asking whether to allow the app to find and connect to devices on your local network.

    Tap **Allow**.

    This permission lets the app discover your Sonos® speakers over Wi-Fi. It does not give the app access to the internet or to any device outside your home network. If you tap **Don't Allow** by mistake, you can re-enable it in **Settings → Somos InControl → Local Network** — see [App Permissions](/getting-started/permissions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant Bluetooth permission (optional)">
    iOS will also ask for Bluetooth permission. This is used only to detect Sonos® speakers that are nearby but not yet connected to your Wi-Fi — for example, a new speaker you're about to set up.

    Tap **Allow** if you want that detection, or **Don't Allow** to skip it. Bluetooth is entirely optional — the app works fully without it for any speaker already on your network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the interactive tour">
    After you grant permissions, a short interactive tour starts automatically. It walks you through the app step by step with highlighted callouts:

    **Welcome → The Menu → Volume → Sound Presets → Fine-tune EQ → Build Your Home Theater → Music & Favorites → Your Rooms → You're All Set**

    You can tap **Skip** at any point and come back to the tour later. Nothing is locked until you finish it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discover your rooms">
    Once the tour is complete (or skipped), the app scans your Wi-Fi and lists every Sonos® room it finds — home theater soundbar rooms, stereo pairs, and single speakers alike. Discovery usually takes a couple of seconds.

    Tap any room to open it and start controlling it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Replay the Tour Anytime

If you skipped the tour or want to see it again, you can replay it from the menu at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Tap the menu button in the **top-left corner** of the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Take the Tour">
    Under the **Help** section, tap **Take the Tour**. The full interactive walkthrough starts from the beginning — Welcome through You're All Set — with the same highlighted callouts as the first time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Gets Discovered

Every Sonos® room on your Wi-Fi network appears in the app automatically — you don't choose rooms manually. What you can control depends on what's in each room:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="All rooms" icon="sliders">
    Volume, bass, treble, loudness, sound presets, Trueplay status, and now-playing info.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Soundbar rooms" icon="tv">
    Everything above, plus height channel, Speech Boost, Night Sound, TV source, and the audio-format badge.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rooms with a subwoofer" icon="volume-high">
    Any room with a bonded subwoofer also shows a subwoofer level slider, regardless of whether it has a soundbar.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your 4 favourite rooms" icon="star">
    The bottom bar holds up to 4 rooms as quick-access tabs. Reorder rooms from the menu (hold and drag) to choose which 4 appear there.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  If a room isn't appearing, make sure your iPhone or iPad is on the **same Wi-Fi network** as your Sonos® speakers. Many routers broadcast separate 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks — your speakers may be on one and your phone on the other.
</Tip>
